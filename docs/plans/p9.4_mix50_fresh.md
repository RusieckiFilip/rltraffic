# P9.4 — the transfer test's second DT retrained on a corrected 50 % mixture

Exploratory, added after the results. Branch `task/p9.4-mix50-fresh`, started at `5eedf21`. This plan is committed, tagged `p9.4-plan` and pushed before any number of this task exists. Nothing from this task goes to `main`, and no existing file under `docs/data/` changes.

## 1. Why, and the label

- The registered CityFlow → SUMO transfer test on hz1x1 has two DT subjects; the second is P4.7's mix50 DT (`PREREGISTRATION.md` line 392, A17(c); `docs/data/p7_3a_zero_shot.json` lines 3182967–3183015, `registered_in` at line 3183530).
- mix50's random half is 100 of its 200 streams, all from `cf_hz1x1__random` (`docs/data/p4_7_declaration.json` lines 4246 and 6057; `offline/method_tier_grid.py` lines 198 and 274–282).
- That collection replays two action schedules: the collector re-created the random generator at every draw (`offline/collect.py` line 981 at `7c18c2d`; A27(d), `PREREGISTRATION.md` line 380). In mix50's random half the two schedules hold 61 and 39 streams (episode files `_seed1001_` and `_seed1000_`).
- A27 kept the three mixtures as the record and trained no mixture on the corrected collection (`offline/method_tier_grid.py` lines 324–347: only `mix_m1000_m500` and `random_fresh`).
- `cf_hz1x1__random_fresh` holds 200 episodes, one per draw 1–200, with 200 distinct schedules (`offline/corrective_round.py` lines 957–960 and 1003–1020; `docs/data/p9_1_random_fresh.json` lines 7510–7516). mix50's random half uses 100 of those draws (2–196).

The label is exploratory and added after the results: the transfer results and A27's results existed when this task was designed. It is not a registered test. A25(c), as A27 amends it, allows no further run, so this task is a deviation from the registered plan and gets a new row in the supplement's deviation table. The same row says that of P7.3a's four prompt arms two are repeated, calibrated (`b_mean_k100`) and source (`naive`), and that the ablation arms `a_q1.0` and `b_max_k100` are not.

## 2. The corpus: only the random half changes

2.1 Selection rule. `offline/method_tier_grid.py::mixture_training_streams` (lines 613–639: expert fraction 0.50, `default_rng(20260813 + 50)`, both pools sorted by stream key), with mix50's expert pool unchanged (mappo1000's 200 training streams) and its random pool replaced by `cf_hz1x1__random_fresh`'s 200 streams. The declaration must show all five, or the task stops before training:

- (i) 200 streams, 100 expert and 100 random;
- (ii) the 100 expert streams equal mix50's 100 by `episode_sha256`;
- (iii) the 100 random streams lie on exactly the 100 flow draws of mix50's random half, one per draw, so the rule equals replacing each random-half stream by the corrected collection's episode on the same draw;
- (iv) the 100 random streams hold 100 distinct per-intersection action sequences;
- (v) each stream's `episode_sha256` equals the sha256 of its file on disk.

2.2 Fitted quantities. The state normalisation and the return scale are computed from the new data by the registered rules, as in A27(d): the registered loader fits the state statistics over the tier's split, which for a mixture is the union of its components' splits, and `rtg_scale` is the training set's largest absolute return. The prompt targets stay unchanged: −5959.0 (source) and −7431.0185327454665 (calibrated). The declaration checks that the registered rule gives the same source target, that is, that the training set's highest return is −5959.0, from the unchanged expert half; if it does not, the task stops.

2.3 What changes besides the generator, recorded in the declaration:

- the normalisation basis: 400 episodes (200 + 200) instead of mix50's 600;
- the return scale: `rtg_scale` 40223.0 in mix50, and the new value;
- the engine seed of the 61 random-half streams collected at engine seed 1001: it is 1000 in the corrected collection (the other 39 were and stay at 1000), read from the corpus manifests.

2.4 `docs/data/p9_4_declaration.json` is committed before the first gradient step. It holds the 200 streams (dataset directory, episode file, flow draw, engine seed, `episode_sha256`, total return), checks (i)–(v), the three changes of 2.3, the normalisation digest, `target_rtg`, `rtg_scale`, the two prompt targets, the recipe, the seeds, the regime, both evaluation designs and the guard subsets.

## 3. Training: mix50's recipe

P4's recipe through the code path that trained mix50: context 20, 3 layers, 1 head, `d_model` 128, dropout 0.1, batch 64, AdamW with learning rate 1e-4 and weight decay 1e-4, 1,000 warm-up steps, gradient clip 0.25, 40,000 steps. Seeds 101, 202, 303, 404 and 505. The deterministic regime in which P5.3c reproduced P4.7's mix50 checkpoints 5 of 5 (`docs/data/p5_3c_train.json`, `k20_reproduction`).

Control first: the registered mix50 DT is retrained, five seeds, at this branch's commit, and every parameter must be `torch.equal` to P4.7's checkpoint (the files pinned by digest in `p7_3a_zero_shot.json`, `inputs.checkpoints`). If fewer than 5 of 5 reproduce, nothing else is trained or evaluated, and the author gets a report: each seed's largest absolute difference and the library versions.

## 4. Evaluation

4.1 SUMO, by P7.3a's rules.

- A15: `E_sumo` is the primary definition and `att_env` is co-reported; SUMO runs teleport-free (`time-to-teleport -1`), with A23's rule for collisions.
- A16: observations enter the model only through `align_info`.
- A17: the two prompt arms of section 1. Calibrated (`b_mean_k100`): Rule B with S = mean and k = 100, computed by `offline/rtg_calibration.py::rule_b_target` from P7.2b's committed statistics; no probe is re-run. Source (`naive`): the training set's highest return. `rtg_scale` is not recalibrated.
- A18(c): one episode per held-out draw 1000–1099, each on a fresh environment at `reset(seed=1000)`; the parity configurations are opened read-only.
- Cells: 2 arms × 5 seeds × 100 draws = 1,000.
- Anchors for all 100 draws are read from `p9_2_sumo_anchors.json`. Registered pair: fixed-time with k = 4 and MaxPressure per-link. Aligned pair: fixed-time at CityFlow's tuned k and MaxPressure counting each lane once. P7.3a's registered anchors equal these values on 100 of 100 draws.

4.2 CityFlow (exploratory). The new DT only: five seeds, held-out draws 1000–1099, P4.7's in-domain recipe and prompt (−5959.0), 500 episodes. The cells are evaluated as P9.1 evaluated its hz1x1 cells: route (i), P4.6's `dt_gate.evaluate_arm`, and route (ii), P8.4b's probe, must agree on `att_ours` under `==`, and every cell carries A11(b)'s five quantities (`att_ours`, `att_engine`, `entered`, `created`, `never_entered`). The original model's cells are P4.7's, as `docs/data/p9_1_iql_targets.json` carries them (`p4_7:dt@mix50`). On hz1x1 `att_engine` is primary (A11(c), Rule R), with `att_ours` beside it.

4.3 Stopping controls. Any difference under `==` stops the stage before its first new cell, and the author gets a report:

- the control of section 3 (5 of 5 checkpoints, parameter for parameter);
- (G1) the registered mix50 DT, both arms, five seeds, draws 1000–1009 (100 SUMO cells), against `p7_3a_zero_shot.json`'s cells (`e_sumo`, `att_env`);
- (G2) the four anchors on draws 1000–1009 (40 SUMO cells), against `p9_2_sumo_anchors.json` (`anchors.sumo.cityflow1x1.by_draw`).

## 5. What is reported

The artifact `docs/data/p9_4_zero_shot.json` keeps P7.3a's format: its cells, rho, h3, contrast and in_support blocks, computed by P7.3a's own functions. It adds the blocks below. The CityFlow records go to `docs/data/p9_4_cityflow.json`, in the format of P9.1's hz1x1 records. The unit is the paired draw: each draw's value is the mean of the five seeds. CI = mean ± 1.96·sd/√100 (`offline/dt_gate.py::mean_ci95`), as in P7.3a. Wilcoxon = `offline/dt_gate.py::wilcoxon_signed_rank`, two-sided. In SUMO everything is on `E_sumo`, with `att_env` beside it, and every number is for the calibrated arm, with the source arm beside it. There is no multiplicity correction, because the task is exploratory.

- R1. DT − fixed-time, for k = 4 and for the tuned k: mean, CI, wins, losses, Wilcoxon p.
- R2. DT − MaxPressure, per-link and lane-once: the same five numbers.
- R3. ρ under the registered and the aligned pair (P7.3a's and P9.2's functions): mean and CI. Readings by the CI: "ρ > 0" if the CI lies above 0, "ρ < 1" if it lies below 1.
- R4. Calibrated − source prompt: the paired difference as in R1, and the difference of mean ρ (P7.3a's contrast block). A17(d)'s registered direction is calibrated ≥ source.
- R5. Corrected − original model (P7.3a's mix50 cells), per arm: the paired difference as in R1, ρ beside it, and whether every reading of R1–R3 agrees.
- R6. CityFlow: the new model's mean ATT under both definitions with its CI over the 100 per-draw means, the per-seed means and A11(b)'s companions; corrected − original (P4.7's cells), paired per draw, as in R1, under both definitions.
- R7. Corrected model − the DT trained offline in SUMO on 200 MaxPressure episodes (`docs/data/p7_3b_anchor.json`, subject `anchor_k200`: 500 cells, draws 1000–1099, engine seed 1000), on `E_sumo`, paired over the 100 draws: mean, CI as in P7.3a, wins and losses. No new run. The two models differ in several things at once (the simulator and the controller that produced their logs, the prompt rule, the alignment of observations), so the difference is reported without a cause.
- Also: per-seed means, decisions in support, vehicles that never entered, collisions.

## 6. Sentences for the paper, one per outcome

The numbers choose the branch; the packet fills the slots: {d} the mean difference in s, [{lo}, {hi}] its CI, {w} and {l} the wins and losses out of 100 draws, {p} the Wilcoxon p, {r} the mean ρ, {m} a mean ATT in s, {n} the vehicles per episode that never entered.

S0, always: "The random half of the 50 % mixture came from the original random collection, whose episodes replay two action schedules. After the transfer results were known, we retrained this DT with the random half drawn from the corrected collection on the same draws. The state normalisation and the return scale were refitted on the new data by the same rules, and the recipe, the prompts and the evaluation stayed the same. We report the retrained model as exploratory and the original one in the supplement."

## 7. Rules for the runs

- Three stages, run by the author in tmux: `train` (GPU), `cityflow` (CPU) and `sumo` (CPU); `cityflow` and `sumo` start only after `train` has passed its control.
- Every session is a few hours at most and has its own token; it ends between units of work at its hour bound or when the STOP file exists, and the next session resumes from disk.
- Every stage runs from a detached worktree at the reviewed commit; a tree with uncommitted changes is refused.
- At most 6 worker processes.
- Outputs only under `output/p9_4/` and `output/p9_4_runs/`; nothing under `output/` is deleted, moved or overwritten.
- Commit messages carry no trailers.

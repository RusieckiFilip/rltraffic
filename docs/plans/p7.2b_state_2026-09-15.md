  "exec bash offline/campaigns/p7_2b_calibration.sh > /tmp/p72b_<run>.log 2>&1"

issued from **a Bash tool call inside the Claude Code session**. What that is and is not:

* it **is** a tmux pane, and the driver was its own **process-group leader** (run 3: pid 1478536,
  pgid 1478536) — which is what Amendment B3's check requires and what `kill -- -$$` needs. The
  `exec` is what makes it true; without it the pane's `sh -c` is the leader, the driver is a child,

  a file. **No human watched any of the three runs**, and no pane survives them (`exec` means the
  pane exits with the driver). That is precisely why runs 1 and 2's canary lines were nearly lost —
  they were printed into panes nobody was attached to and which no longer exist — and why
  `canary.log` was added in E1.2.

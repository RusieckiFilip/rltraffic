## 1. The question, and the three claims that are registered before the data

`P5.3a` settled that the prompt is **a strong lever on the policy** (499 of 500 held-out episodes
move between targets `0` and `−13000`, mean absolute 2.1281 ATT) and **a weak one on mean quality**
(+0.9026). So the knob exists. **What is unproven is that it is worth anything**, and that is this
task's question:

> **Does removing the knob cost anything, and does the cost track the corpus's between-episode
> return spread?**

### 1.1 The tier set — fixed by a rule registered before the numbers existed

`BRIEF_28` §9 registered the rule on 2026-08-24: *"(i) `mappo1000`, the headline tier; (ii) the tier
with the largest measured between-episode scaled-RTG sd; (iii) the tier with the smallest."*
`P5.3a`'s **row B** resolved it. The values below are **copied** from
`docs/data/p5_3a_rtg_probe.json` and are **re-evaluated by a test** (§6, test 6) rather than trusted
as constants: the test reads all eight tiers' row-B pooled scaled sd out of the committed artifact,
applies the rule, and asserts it returns exactly these three.

| tier | row B pooled (scaled) | committed `dt` ATT | role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mix50` | **0.2725** | 107.7026 | **widest spread** |
| `mappo1000` | 0.0391 | 104.9558 | declared headline tier |
| `random` | **0.0048** | 420.3764 | **narrowest**; P5.3a measured its DT at 0 of 7200 flips |

**3 tiers × 5 seeds = 15 `dt_nortg` cells. Nothing else trains** except §2's single control cell.

⚠️ **The three `dt` ATTs differ by 4×.** Half the reason §4 forbids a single equivalence threshold,
and the reason §7 item 3 is in the packet.

### 1.2 ⛔ NO EQUIVALENCE THRESHOLD, ANYWHERE

`PREREGISTRATION` A7 withdrew the per-tier δ rule on 2026-08-25 because it spanned eleven orders of
magnitude and could not return one of its answers on part of its domain. **This task does not
reinvent it and issues no equivalence verdict.** It reports the paired difference, its 95 % CI, the
per-seed reversals, and the tier's own `dt` ATT beside them so the reader can scale it.

> ⚠️ **A CI containing 0 is a failure to reject, never a demonstration of equivalence** (A6's own
> words). Every sentence about a null result in the artifact and the packet is written that way.
> `assert_no_verdicts` (`offline/method_tier_grid.py:1838`) guards the payload, and a **text scan of
> the shipped bytes** for `equivalent`, `within_delta`, `matches`, `verdict`, `inert`, `no
> difference`, `threshold` and `delta` is a committed test.

### 3.1 Sign convention, fixed now

`mean_difference = mean(ATT_dt − ATT_dt_nortg)` over shared draws, i.e. **left = `dt`, right =
`dt_nortg`**, matching Q2's literal wording. **Lower ATT is better**, so a **negative** difference
means the prompted arm is better and a **positive** one means removing the prompt helped.

### 3.2 Q1's quantity is ambiguous in the brief; the disambiguation is fixed here, before the data

*"The paired absolute difference"* admits two readings. **Registered: `abs(mean_difference)` — the
magnitude of the paired mean — is the PRIMARY and the only scored quantity**, because it is the
quantity §1.2's CI is about and the quantity Q2 tests, so the two predictions stay on one scale.
`mean(|per-draw difference|)` **is also computed and reported**, as an explicitly **secondary,
unregistered** ordering; if the two orderings disagree that disagreement is itself reported, and the
verdict follows the primary.

> ✅ **CONFIRMED BY AMENDMENT A2, 2026-08-27**, which also supplies a fact the packet must carry:
> ⭐ **scoring Q1 on the RAW ATT scale is CONSERVATIVE.** The three `dt` ATTs are 105 · 108 · **420**.
> If paired differences scale at all with baseline ATT, `random` would show the **largest** raw
> difference — the **opposite** of what Q1 predicts. **The registered scale therefore makes Q1 harder
> to confirm, not easier**, and §3.3's normalised column is a reading aid rather than an escape
> hatch. Switching to it after seeing the result remains forbidden by §3.3's own sentence.

### 3.3 A scale-normalised ordering is reported and is NOT a registered claim

Because the three tiers' `dt` ATTs differ by 4× (105 · 108 · 420), the artifact also carries
`abs(mean_difference) / att_dt_mean` per tier. **It is labelled `secondary_not_registered`.** Q1 is
scored on the raw scale exactly as `BRIEF_30` §3 registers it; the normalised column exists so a
reader can see what the scale does, and switching to it after seeing the result is forbidden by this
sentence.

### 3.4 Per-seed reversals, defined now

For tier `T` and seed `s`, `d_s = mean_over_draws(ATT_dt(s, ·)) − mean_over_draws(ATT_dt_nortg(s, ·))`.
With `D` the pooled `mean_difference`, **`seeds_reversed = #{ s : d_s * D <= 0.0 }`** — an exact zero
counts as a reversal, which is the conservative direction. Reported per tier with all five `d_s`.
⚠️ **`d_s` averages draws inside a seed while `D` averages seeds inside a draw**, so the two are the
same quantity in exact arithmetic and may differ in the last bits. The artifact records
`mean_of_per_seed_differences` beside `mean_difference` and their **measured** difference; **no
equality is asserted between them**, because that would condemn a correct implementation.

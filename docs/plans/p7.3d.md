## 26. B.7.5's ruling, applied — and the choices made while implementing it

Ruled after `git merge --no-edit main` → `af2ce8e`: **Q-a YES** (B.7.2-1 and B.7.2-2 in B.7.4's one commit), **Q-b NO**
(no `local_return_convention` field), **Q-c YES** (null ρ_i with the reason, the diagnostic still written). Also from
`main`: the G4 re-review's minor 8(a) — the reroll comparison test's presence loop names `"actions"`, one line in this
commit (`docs/reviews/P7.3d-preflight-2.md`, the coordinator's ruling, item 4).

Choices, all inside the ruling:
- **The block's shape.** `per_intersection_rho.per_intersection[i]` = `{rho, reason, mean_return {b_mean_k100,
  fixedtime, maxpressure}, n_draws}`, and `per_intersection_rho.denominator_diagnostic` = `{what_this_is,
  per_intersection[i]}`, B.7.2-2's own path. `n_intersections_rho_null` and `intersection_ids_rho_null` are the header's
  count (B.7.5-4).
- **Float route, fixed in the code.** Seed means within a draw in seed order, then 100-draw means in draw order, by
  `sum()/len()`; `rho()` for the ratio, after an exactly-zero check; `mean_ci95`'s primitives for the gap's mean and
  its SD. `denominator` (a difference of means) and `mean_gap` (a mean of differences) are the same quantity by two routes
  and may differ in the last bits; both are reported as computed.
- **A refusal the ruling implies.** A draw with DT cells but not both anchors, or the reverse, refuses: the three means
  would be over different draws. `report`'s completeness check makes it unreachable there.
- **The pin can fail.** The complete-set fixture's per-intersection gaps vary across draws (32/64/96/128). B2 ties on 10
  draws and is 32 worse on 10 (20 not-better draws); C3's mean gap is exactly zero, so its ρ is null. On every non-null
  intersection the superseded mean of per-draw ratios is more than 1e-3 away from the ratio of means, and the test
  asserts that. A first B2 pattern (worse on `k % 10 == 3`) separated the two by one ULP only; it was replaced before any
  implementation, found by a scratch computation.
- **A unit test** pins the block on four hand-worked draws. One draw has three seeds, so "seeds within a draw first" is a
  different number from a grand mean over cells.

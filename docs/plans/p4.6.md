## 3. Design

### 3.1 The grid

**Phase 1 (the deliverable):** `{bc, bc_top10, iql, dt} x {random, fixedtime, maxpressure, mappo500}`
= **16 new cells**, plus the four `mappo1000` cells **re-used under the gate of section 7**.

**Out of scope, stated so it cannot drift in:** `mappo200`, `mappo060`, any other scenario, SUMO,
CQL, any change to the DT architecture, any change to `agent/DTAgent.py`,
`agent/OfflineBaselines.py`, `offline/dataset.py` or `offline/dt_gate.py`.

⚠️ **Ordering rule.** Tiers are ordered by **measured behaviour ATT** in every table, figure and
sentence: `mappo1000` (105.4614) < `mappo500` (107.4956) < `maxpressure` (176.5001) <
`fixedtime` (262.0887) < `random` (422.5188). Never by tier name, never by training budget. The
artifact carries this order as data (`tier_order_by_behaviour_att`) so a reader cannot reconstruct a
different one by accident.

### 3.2 Size matching (brief section 7.1 and 7.2) — the confound this design exists to kill

- **Every tier trains on exactly 200 streams = 72,000 windows.** Asserted from the artifact, per tier,
  not argued in prose.
- `random` holds **400** streams (2 per draw). It is subsampled to 200 by a **declared, stratified
  rule**: **exactly one of the two episodes of each of the 200 draws**, chosen by
  `numpy.random.default_rng(20260813)` over the draw's candidates sorted by `(dataset_dir,
  episode_file)`, draws visited in ascending order.
  **Why stratified rather than a flat 200-of-400 draw:** every other tier covers each of the 200
  demand draws exactly once, so one-per-draw matches the tiers on **demand coverage** as well as on
  count. A flat subsample would give some draws two episodes and others none, which is a second
  difference between arms and precisely the class of confound section 7.1 of the brief is about.
- The full selection (200 `(dataset_dir, episode_file, flow_draw, return)` records) is written into
  `docs/data/p4_6_declaration.json`, so the arm is reproducible from the artifact alone.
- **%BC** takes the top **10 %** of its tier's training set **by stream return** — 20 streams,
  7,200 windows — the same `TOP_RETURN_FRACTION = 0.10` filter P4.4 declared, unchanged.

### 3.3 The per-tier statistics rule (brief section 7.3), declared once and applied uniformly

> **Every per-tier statistic is fitted on that tier's FULL training split (its draws 1-200, every
> episode it contains). The training SET is the size-matched 200-stream subsample of that split.**

The statistics this covers: state normalisation (`NormalizationStats`), the RTG summary and hence
`rtg_scale = max(|rtg_min|, |rtg_max|)`, the DT's naive `target_rtg`, and IQL's `reward_scale`.

- For `fixedtime`, `maxpressure`, `mappo500` and `mappo1000` the split and the training set are the
  **same 200 streams**, so the rule is a no-op and P4.4's `mappo1000` path is reproduced exactly
  (verified in advance for the reward scale, section 2.3).
- For `random` the two differ: statistics come from 400 streams, training rows from 200.
  **Consequence, declared:** the DT's naive target for `random` is **-38369.0** (the split maximum),
  which may belong to an episode outside the training subsample; the largest possible discrepancy is
  bounded by the subsample maxima measured during exploration (section 12.1) and is at most **364**
  units of return on a scale whose `rtg_scale` is **40294**, i.e. **0.9 %**.
- **Statistics are never shared across tiers.** A shared statistic would be a leak between arms
  (brief section 7.3). Each tier's checkpoints carry their own tier's statistics, and the artifact
  records a sha256 digest of each tier's serialised statistics so two tiers can be shown to differ.

### 3.4 Budgets, seeds, evaluation (brief section 7.4) — no arm gets what the others do not

- **40,000 gradient steps** for every method, every tier, every seed. Declared here, before training;
  **no raise is available** to this task (P4.4's `raise_available: false` discipline). P4's DT reached
  40,000 through its own declared 20,000 -> 40,000 raise, so the re-used DT column is at the same
  budget as everything trained here.
- **Training seeds** `(101, 202, 303, 404, 505)` — `dt_gate.TRAINING_SEEDS`, unchanged.
- **Evaluation:** `dt_gate.evaluate_arm` unchanged, one episode per draw, over the **100 registered

  a corpus manifest (section 2.2). **n = 500 per cell.**
- **Batch sizes** are P4.4's and P4's: 64 windows for BC/%BC, 64 windows for the DT, 1,280
  transitions for IQL. **IQL hyper-parameters are P4.4's published-D4RL constants, untouched and
  untuned** (`tau 0.7`, `beta 3.0`, `gamma 0.99`, `polyak 0.005`, `weight_clip 100.0`), and P4.4's
  analytic refusal to sweep them is inherited verbatim: the authorised criterion cannot rank either
  parameter it is authorised to rank.
- **Threads pinned everywhere** (`DEFERRED` 41): `--torch-threads 1` on every job plus
  `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1` in the environment. Recorded in every artifact through
  `runtime_provenance`.
- **Device:** the default (`Utils.resolve_device(None)` -> `cuda` here), which is what P4, P4.3 and
  P4.4 used. The gate of section 7 is what *proves* the configuration matches theirs; a mismatch is a

## 4. The registered predictions (brief section 6), VERBATIM, and how each is scored

> **P1 — BC's rank among the four methods worsens as tier quality falls.** Basis: OffLight's Table II
> (read first-hand) has BC **worst of the offline methods in all nine cells** on a six-controller
> heterogeneous mixture, while our P4.4 has BC **matching** the DT on pure expert. Those are the two
> endpoints; this predicts the interior.
>
> **P2 — %BC's advantage over BC is LARGEST on the heterogeneous mixtures and SMALLEST on `random`.**
> On a mixture the top-decile filter selects the expert fraction; on pure random there is no expert
> fraction to find.
>
> **P3 (leakage-free, and it costs no training) — on `random`, %BC's top decile carries a DEMAND
> signature; on `mappo1000` it did not.** P4.5 established the pure-expert instantiation: the filter
> selected the two best *checkpoints*, arrivals were equal (443.6 vs 446.1) and only 4/20 of the kept
> episodes overlapped MaxPressure's difficulty ranking. **Run the identical two checks per tier.**

**These three are one hypothesis with three instantiations:** *the top-decile filter selects the best
behaviour MODE available, whatever a mode is in that corpus* — best **seeds** on pure expert
(measured), best **controller** on a mixture, easiest **demand draws** on pure random.

### 4.1 P1 — scoring rule, and the degeneracy that must be disclosed first

⚠️ **Disclosed before the rule, because it constrains it:** on the anchor tier `mappo1000` the four
committed cells are `bc_top10` 103.1627 < `iql` 103.4764 < `dt` 104.9558 < `bc` 105.1635, so
**BC's rank there is already 4 of 4 — the worst possible.** "Worsens as quality falls" therefore
cannot be observed as an increase from the anchor; the sentence reduces, given the anchor, to *BC
stays worst everywhere as quality falls*. That is still falsifiable, in one direction, and it is
falsified the moment BC beats any of the other three on any tier.

- **Rank definition:** rank 1 = lowest `att_horizon` cell mean among `{bc, bc_top10, iql, dt}` within
  a tier; 4 = highest. Exact ties take the average rank and force the outcome **NOT RESOLVED** (two
  float means being exactly equal has never happened in this project; the branch exists so that it
  cannot be decided after the fact).
- **PRIMARY (declared):** **P1 HOLDS** iff BC's rank is **4 on every one of the five tiers**.
  **P1 FAILS** iff BC's rank is < 4 on at least one tier. The full rank sequence in quality order is
  reported either way.
- **SECONDARY, reported beside it and carrying no verdict:** the continuous form of "worsens" —
  `bc_gap(tier) = mean_att(bc) - min over the other three methods`, plus Kendall's tau-b between the
  quality index (1..5, `mappo1000` = 1) and `bc_gap`. **No threshold is attached to tau-b**; it is
  reported because a rank that is pinned at its floor compresses away the magnitude, which is exactly
  the failure mode `PROJECT_PLAN` section 1 records for verdicts generally.

### 4.2 P2 — scoring rule, and what phase 1 alone can and cannot score

- **Advantage definition:** `adv(tier) = mean_att(bc) - mean_att(bc_top10)`, positive when %BC is
  better, reported with the **paired 95 % CI** of that within-tier contrast (the same 100 draws).
- **FULL scoring (needs phase 2):** **P2 HOLDS** iff `adv` attains its **minimum** over all eight
  tiers at `random` **and** its **maximum** at one of the three mixture tiers. **FAILS** otherwise.
- **PHASE-1-ONLY partial scoring, declared now so it cannot be invented later:** report `adv` for all
  five single-controller tiers with CIs, and score only the clause phase 1 can see — *`random`
  attains the minimum among the five single-controller tiers* — reported as **P2-partial HOLDS /
  FAILS**, with the explicit sentence that **the mixture half of P2 is unscorable without phase 2 and
  no verdict is issued on it**. Anchor already known: `adv(mappo1000) = 105.1635 - 103.1627 =
  2.0008`.

### 4.3 P3 — the two checks, defined mechanically (identical per tier)

Both run on **training data only** (draws 1-200) and cost **no rollout and no gradient step**. The
selection they interrogate is that tier's %BC top decile: **20 kept streams of 200**.

**Check A — volume (the "arrivals" check).** `arrivals(draw)` is the number of vehicles in that
draw's demand, obtained by rebuilding the corpus's own `FlowRandomizer` from the manifest's
`flow_source_path` + `flow_randomizer_params` and calling `draw(d).n_vehicles` **in memory** (no
files written). ⚠️ **The reconstruction is verified, not assumed:** the rendered CityFlow bytes'
sha256 must equal the manifest's recorded `flow_draw_sha256[d]` for **every one of the 200 draws**, or
the check refuses to report. Statistic: `delta_arrivals = mean(arrivals | kept 20) - mean(arrivals |
other 180)`, with a 95 % interval from the two-sample (Welch) standard error at z = 1.96, **labelled
as a normal approximation**.

**Check B — difficulty (the "MaxPressure ranking" check).** Difficulty of a draw is the
`att_horizon` of the **`cf_hz1x1__maxpressure`** episode of that draw (one per draw, all 200
present). "Easiest 20" = the 20 draws with the **lowest** MaxPressure ATT. Statistic:
`overlap = |kept draws ∩ easiest 20|`, whose null is hypergeometric(N = 200, K = 20, n = 20) with
mean **2.0**; the reported p-value is the **exact** upper tail `P(X >= overlap)` computed with
`math.comb` (integer arithmetic, no RNG, no new dependency).

- **Scoring:** **P3 HOLDS on a tier** iff Check B's exact p < 0.05 **or** Check A's 95 % interval
  excludes 0. The prediction proper is about **`random`**; the same two numbers are computed and
  reported for **every** tier including `mappo1000`, whichever way they come out.
- **No multiplicity correction is applied**, and that is a declared position, not an oversight: P3's
  scoring rule is fixed on one tier (`random`) in advance; the other four columns are descriptive.
- **`mappo1000` is the control, and it is a check on my instrument as much as on the corpus:** the
  coordinator's ad-hoc figures were **443.6 vs 446.1** arrivals and **4/20** overlap. If my
  implementation disagrees with either, **the disagreement is reported and diagnosed**, and neither
  number is silently adopted.

## 5. The DT's prompt: one declared rule per tier, no sweep

> **Each tier's DT is conditioned on that tier's naive target: `max(training-split episode return)`.**

Declared values, computed from the corpus during planning (section 2.1) and **asserted by the code
against the data before training**:

| tier | declared `target_rtg` | `rtg_scale` = max(&#124;min&#124;, &#124;max&#124;) |
|---|---|---|
| `mappo1000` | **-5762.0** | 9991.0 |
| `mappo500` | **-6362.0** | 11043.0 |
| `maxpressure` | **-13112.0** | 24115.0 |
| `fixedtime` | **-29707.0** | 33225.0 |
| `random` | **-38369.0** | 40294.0 |

`mappo1000`'s pair is exactly what `docs/data/p4_dt_config.json` records for P4, and that file already
states the rule in the same words: *"target_rtg = max episode return in the training split;
rtg_scale = max|rtg|"*. **This task adopts P4's registered rule; it does not invent one.**

**Why no sweep, recorded because the compute budget rests on it:** P4.3 measured a **13,000-wide**
declared grid on `mappo1000` and the DT's held-out ATT moved **0.9026** end to end, with only **3 of
8** adjacent steps resolving and **none of the 5 interior ones**. A per-tier sweep would cost roughly
10x this task's evaluation budget to chase an effect smaller than the between-method differences the
grid exists to measure, and P4.3's boundary corollary says a grid may not be extended because its
edge won. **One declared point per tier, no sweep, no post-hoc adjustment.**

⚠️ **Known limitation, stated here and repeated in the packet:** on a weak tier the naive rule asks
the model to achieve *that tier's* best return, which may be poor in absolute terms — the DT on
`random` is prompted at -38369, a target its own data says is achievable and that a good controller
would consider terrible. **That is the correct behaviour of the naive rule and is exactly what the
ladder is testing.** It must not be read as a tuned prompt, and no claim of prompt calibration
attaches to any cell in this grid.

## 6. NO EQUIVALENCE VERDICTS — the ruling, and what is reported instead

Brief section 4 rules it and this plan implements it mechanically:

> **P4.6 reports paired mean differences, their 95 % CIs, the CI WIDTH and the rank-biserial effect
> size for every pair, and issues NO equivalence verdict anywhere.**

- A6's `delta = 0.6263` is **`mappo1000`-specific** (the DT's measured margin over its behaviour
  policy *on that tier*), and there is no non-circular way to derive a per-tier delta before the run.
- **Mechanically enforced:** `offline/method_tier_grid.py` does **not** import
  `equivalence_verdict`, `delta_verdict`, `DELTA_ATT` or any `VERDICT_*` constant, and a test asserts
  (a) that the module's source contains no such reference and (b) that the emitted artifact contains
  no key or string value matching `verdict`/`within_delta`/`matches`/`equivalent`.
- **What every pair gets instead:** `mean_difference`, `ci95_low`, `ci95_high`, `ci95_half_width`,
  **`ci95_width`**, `median_difference`, `wins/losses/ties`, `rank_biserial`, the Wilcoxon statistic
  and p-value, and `n_shared_draws` — all from the existing, tested `paired_comparison`.
- **Which pairs:** **all 6 within-tier method pairs on each of the 5 tiers (30)** and **all 10
  within-method tier pairs for each of the 4 methods (40)** = **70 paired comparisons** in phase 1.
  All 70 are over the same 100 held-out draws, so all are genuinely paired.
- **Multiplicity:** none applied, declared: no decision rule is attached to any of the 70. The only
  declared decisions in this task are P1, P2-partial and P3, each with its own rule fixed in section
  4 before any number exists.
- **`recovered_fraction` is not reported per tier.** It is defined relative to the DT's margin over
  its behaviour policy, which is a `mappo1000` quantity; extending it per tier would smuggle in the
  same circularity section 4 of the brief refuses. The behaviour policy's own ATT per tier is
  reported instead, as a plain reference column (section 2.1's ladder means).

### 2.4 🚨 "cadence is zero by construction on SUMO" is a premise, not a fact — I will measure it

`BRIEF_34` §2.1 declares the cadence term *"zero by construction on SUMO (exact departures and
per-second arrivals); assert `W_sumo == att_sumo_env` under `==` on every episode"*. Reading
`metrics/sumo.py`, the arrival *times* are per-second (`on_sim_step` buffers `getArrivedIDList` with
`getTime()`, `:244-253`) but the **population bookkeeping runs at the `update()` cadence, once per
decision step**: `depart_time[vid]` is filled only for vehicles present in `getIDList()` at a decision
boundary (`:422-426`). A vehicle that departs **and** arrives strictly inside one 10 s window is never
snapshotted, hits the `seen is None` branch (`:389-394`), and is credited
`real_tt = max(0, t_arr − depart_time.pop(vid, t_arr)) = 0.0` **while still incrementing the
denominator**. There is also a second divergence source: a vehicle removed by SUMO without arriving
(teleport-remove) stays out of both the completed list and `getIDList()` and so leaves the env's ATT
entirely, while a naive per-second `W` would still credit it.

**So SUMO's ATT has a cadence artefact of its own, of exactly the shape A12 found on CityFlow.** I
will not assume it is zero and I will not assume it is non-zero: the observer **counts**
`n_arrived_never_observed_at_a_boundary` and `n_vanished_without_arrival` per episode, the record
carries a `term_cadence = att_sumo_env − W_sumo`, and the report states `n_zero / n`. The brief's
assertion becomes a **measured criterion with a count**, which is what A13 says a criterion must be.
If it is 0.0 on all 15, the brief's sentence is confirmed by measurement rather than by construction;
if not, the freeze reports a three-term decomposition on SUMO exactly as on CityFlow. **Either outcome
is reportable and neither is a defect in the instrument.**

### 2.5 The pool identity's exact form

`BRIEF_34` §2.1: *"assert that the union of departed ∪ pending ∪ not-yet-due equals the file's id set
at every second"*. Taken literally with an instantaneous `departed`, this is false the second a vehicle
arrives. The sound form, which is what I will implement and test:

```
cumulative_departed(<=t)  ∪  pending_now(t)  ∪  {v : intended_depart(v) > t}  ==  file_ids
```

with the three sets pairwise disjoint, checked every simulated second, with a positive control that
dropping one id is caught.

### 4.1 Layer A — the reconstruction (imports stdlib, numpy, `envs`, `experiments` only)

```python
read_intended_departures(rou_path) -> dict[str, float]
    # stdlib ElementTree over <vehicle id= depart=>. Refuses: a duplicate id; a non-numeric
    # depart ("triggered"); the presence of <flow>/<trip> (ids are not enumerable from them).

class SumoObservationRecorder:                    # twin of EngineObservationRecorder
    def __init__(self, *, delta_time: float, intended_departures: Mapping[str, float]) -> None
    def observe(self, *, sim_time, present_ids, departed_ids, arrived_ids,
                pending_ids, teleport_start_ids, halting_by_lane=None,
                speed_halting_by_lane=None) -> None
        # refuses non-increasing sim_time; refuses an arrival of an id never departed;
        # refuses a departed id absent from the route file; checks section 2.5's identity
        # EVERY second and raises naming the offending ids.
    # properties: departed_at, arrived_at, first_seen/last_seen windows, n_pending_at_horizon,
    #             n_teleports, halting_max_abs_difference, n_halting_seconds_checked,
    #             n_arrived_never_observed_at_a_boundary, n_vanished_without_arrival
```

Four reconstructions, named to read across to Gate 0's:

| name | population | clock origin | contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| `E_sumo` | every `v` with `intended_depart(v) ≤ T` | route-file `depart` | `(arrival(v) or T) − intended_depart(v)` |
| `P_sumo` | every `v` departed by `T` | route-file `depart` | same |
| `W_sumo` | every `v` departed by `T` | **actual** `getDeparture(v)` | `(arrival(v) or T) − actual_depart(v)` |
| `att_sumo_env` | the env's own `info["average_travel_time"]` at the horizon | — | the reference, read not rebuilt |

Terms, each reported per episode, mirroring `GateEpisode`'s properties:
`term_population = P − E` · `term_clock_origin = W − P` · `term_cadence = att_sumo_env − W` ·
`decomposition_residual = |(population + clock_origin + cadence) − (att_sumo_env − E)|`, which is
**exactly zero by algebra** and is therefore a *self-check of the arithmetic*, reported per episode.

**The second route, required by `CLAUDE.md` §2 and by `BRIEF_34` §2.1:** `term_clock_origin` must equal
`−mean(getDepartDelay(v))` over the departed population, computed from per-vehicle
`traci.vehicle.getDepartDelay` queried **once, at the second the vehicle departs**, and asserted to
`1e-9` against the summation-order-independent route. A second, stricter one is free: the observed
departure second must equal `getDeparture(v)` exactly, so
`actual_depart` is cross-checked against the observation grid under `==`.

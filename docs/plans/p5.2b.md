## 3. The defect, as the record shows it

- **Mechanism.** `_run_train_baselines` filters BC's and %BC's batches to the declared streams (`filter_stacked_to_streams`,
  `tier_sweep.py:2038`) but builds IQL's table with `build_transitions(dataset, …)` (`:2078`), which iterates every

  episodes and the declaration selects 200 (one per draw, RNG 20,260,818), so IQL trained on 400 × 16 × 360 = 2,304,000
  transitions while every other arm trained on the selected 1,152,000.
- **Why the record hid it.** The provenance's `training_streams` is `len(streams)`, the selected 3,200 (`:2056`); the reward
  scale comes from the selected streams too (`:2036`, V2), so both recorded numbers describe the declared set.
- **Rows.** 2,304,000 in all five original checkpoints (V1); 1,152,000 in every other IQL and BC checkpoint of P5.2 that I read.
- **What the artifact records about it.** File and line at both commits (`:1820` at `9460800`, `:2078` at `f077875`); each
  original checkpoint's path, sha256 (from `SHA256SUMS_p5_2.txt`) and `training_rows`; the declared counts from the
  declaration and the corpus manifest; and the fix's location.

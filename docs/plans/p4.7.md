## 0. What is measured, in one sentence

**Three corpora that hold data quality's two components apart** — 33 / 50 / 67 % expert streams mixed
with random streams at **constant state coverage and constant size** — trained with the same four
offline methods, the same five seeds, the same 40,000 gradient steps and the same 100 held-out draws
as P4.6's five single-controller tiers, **so that a C1 ranking can for the first time be attributed to
composition rather than to quality-and-coverage moving together.**

## 3. Design

### 3.1 The three tiers

| tier | expert streams | random streams | total | expert dirs | random dir |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `mix33` | 66 | 134 | 200 | the five `cf_hz1x1__mappo1000__seed{101,202,303,404,505}` | `cf_hz1x1__random` |
| `mix50` | 100 | 100 | 200 | " | " |
| `mix67` | 134 | 66 | 200 | " | " |

- **Components are the two grid tiers' own declared training sets** — `mappo1000`'s 200 streams and
  `random`'s size-matched one-per-draw 200 — so a mixture is literally a composition of two arms
  already measured, and no collection, rollout or new corpus is involved.
- **Selection:** `numpy.random.default_rng(MIXTURE_RNG_BASE + round(100 × fraction))`, i.e.
  **20260846 / 20260863 / 20260880**, choosing without replacement from each component sorted by
  `(dataset_dir, episode_file, ix_id)`. Counts `round(200 × fraction)` = **66 / 100 / 134**.
- **Size-matched at 200 streams**, asserted from the artifact by `assert_equal_training_size`.

### 3.2 The inherited confound, stated and not fixed here (`BRIEF_19` §5.2, `DEFERRED` 28)

`mappo1000`'s five seeds occupy **disjoint contiguous 40-draw blocks** (`seed101`→1–40 … `seed505`→161–200).
A mixture's expert component therefore carries a **seed-by-demand confound it did not create**, and
seed and demand block cannot be separated within it. **The declaration records, per mixture, which
seeds and which draw blocks the expert component drew from**, and the packet reports it. It is not
fixed here and no sentence may claim it was.

**Realised, measured 2026-08-15 (the training set's composition — NOT the top decile, which is Q1's
quantity and is not computed until after this plan is committed):**

|---|---|---|---|---|
| `mix33` | `{101:12, 202:11, 303:14, 404:13, 505:16}` | 66 distinct, 4–200 | 134 distinct, 2–199 | **39** |
| `mix50` | `{101:16, 202:22, 303:20, 404:15, 505:27}` | 100 distinct, 2–199 | 100 distinct, 2–196 | **48** |

that works on *sets* of draw ids sees 152 units where the tier holds 200 streams.

### 3.3 Statistics, and the one asymmetry a reader must be told about

Phase 1's rule applies unchanged: **normalisation statistics are fitted by the loader over the tier's
full training split; the prompt target, the RTG scale and IQL's reward scale are computed over the
tier's training SET.** For a mixture the split is the union of the six directories — **600 episodes**
(200 expert + 400 random) — while the training set is the composed 200. This is the same shape as
phase 1's `random` tier (split 400, set 200) and is declared, not incidental. **No statistic is ever
shared across tiers.**

### 3.4 Budgets — no arm gets what the others do not

draws 1000–1099**, same `evaluate_arm`, same `engine_seed=1000`, same env settings asserted identical
across every contributing manifest. **12 new cells** (3 tiers × 4 methods), 60 new checkpoints.

## 4. ⭐ REGISTERED PREDICTIONS — verbatim from `BRIEF_19` §4, with the rule that scores each

**P4.6's three registered predictions all failed and one was then rescued by a narrative that did not
survive review. These are expected to fail too, and no rescue will be built.**

> **Q1 — the filter FINDS the expert fraction: on all three mixtures, ≥ 90 % of %BC's kept top-decile
> streams come from the `mappo1000` dirs.** This is the direct test of R2's mechanism and it is
> leakage-free — it costs no training and reads only the selection.
>
> **Q2 — %BC's advantage over BC is POSITIVE on all three mixtures, and DECREASES as the expert
> fraction RISES.** At 33 % BC's training set is most diluted, so the filter has the most to recover;
> at 67 % BC is already mostly cloning expert data.
>
> **Q3 — the kept set's COMPOSITION signature is strong where P4.6's DEMAND signature was absent.**
> Run `BRIEF_17` §6's two P3 diagnostics unchanged per mixture tier. Prediction: the composition split
> separates sharply while the arrival-volume and MaxPressure-difficulty checks stay near null —
> **i.e. on a mixture the filter selects MODE, not DIFFICULTY.** ⚠️ **A null composition signature
> would falsify R2 outright and is the most informative outcome available here.**

### 4.1 Q1 — scoring rule

- **Quantity:** `kept_expert(tier) = |{s ∈ top_decile_streams(training_streams(tier)) : s.dataset_dir
  ends with one of the five mappo1000 seed dirs}|`, over `|kept| = ceil(0.10 × 200) = 20`.
- **HELD** iff `kept_expert ≥ 18` (i.e. ≥ 90 % of 20) on **all three** mixtures. **FAILED** if any tier
  is below 18. No tie is possible: the statistic is an integer against an integer threshold.
- **Companion, no threshold:** the exact hypergeometric upper tail `P(X ≥ kept_expert)` under
  "20 streams drawn uniformly from the 200", population 200, successes 66/100/134 — the same
  `hypergeometric_upper_tail` P3 check B uses — plus the **behaviour-seed histogram** of the kept set
  (`BRIEF_17` §11 A5's second axis), so "the filter selected the expert fraction" is never confounded
  with the checkpoint selection P4.5 already established.
- **Disclosure fixed in advance:** if the two components' return distributions do not overlap, Q1 is
  near-tautological. **It is therefore reported beside a measured overlap statistic** — the fraction of
  random-component streams whose return exceeds the *minimum* expert-component return, and the
  rank-based common-language effect size between the two components' returns. This is the same move
  P4.6's review forced on `maxpressure`'s circular check (ρ = −0.9967), applied **before** the number
  is seen rather than after.

### 4.2 Q2 — scoring rule

- **Quantity:** `advantage(tier) = mean ATT(bc@tier) − mean ATT(bc_top10@tier)` over the 500 held-out
  episodes of each cell (positive = %BC better, since lower ATT is better). Identical in definition to
  `score_p2`'s `advantage_by_tier`, which is where it is read from.
- **HELD** iff `advantage > 0` on all three mixtures **AND** `advantage(mix33) > advantage(mix50) >
  advantage(mix67)` strictly. **FAILED** otherwise. **NOT RESOLVED** if any two are exactly equal.
- **Companion, no threshold and no verdict:** the paired `bc − bc_top10` difference per mixture with
  its 95 % CI, CI **width** and rank-biserial, taken from `grid_comparisons`, over the 100 shared draws.

### 4.3 Q3 — scoring rule

Three checks per mixture tier, each reported whichever way it comes out.

- **Composition check (new, exact):** `p_comp = hypergeometric_upper_tail(200, n_expert, 20,
  kept_expert)`. **Signature present iff `p_comp < 0.05`** (`P3_ALPHA`, inherited unchanged).
- **Demand checks (unchanged from `BRIEF_17` §6 / `score_p3`'s per-tier rule):** `volume_check` on
  arrivals and `difficulty_check` against the `maxpressure` tier's per-draw ATT.
  **Demand signature present iff the difficulty p-value < 0.05 OR the volume 95 % interval excludes
  zero.**
- **Q3 HELD** iff, on **all three** mixtures, the composition signature is present **and** the demand
  signature is absent. **FAILED** otherwise. Per-tier sub-outcomes are reported regardless.
- ⚠️ **Disclosure fixed in advance:** on a mixture the same `flow_draw` can appear in both components,
  so `volume_check`'s kept/other sets are sets of **draw ids** and lose multiplicity. **The number of
  draws appearing in both components is reported per tier**, and if it is large the volume check is
  reported as weakened rather than as null.

## 5. ⚖️ REGISTERED — the CONSTRUCTED behaviour reference (`BRIEF_19` §3)

**A 33 %-expert corpus is a composition, not a policy anyone ran**, so there is nothing to roll out and
no rollout will be run. The reference is **built the same way the corpus is built**:

- **RNG:** `numpy.random.default_rng([CONSTRUCTED_REFERENCE_RNG_SEED, round(100 × fraction), seed])`
  with **`CONSTRUCTED_REFERENCE_RNG_SEED = 20_260_814`** (the repo's date convention), one independent
  stream per `(tier, seed)`.
- **Assignment:** for each of the **5 seeds**, exactly **`round(100 × fraction)` = 33 / 50 / 67** of the
  100 held-out draws are assigned to the `mappo1000` cell and the remainder to the `random` cell,
  chosen without replacement from `sorted(HELD_OUT_DRAWS)`.
- **Values:** the **stored per-draw record** of the assigned component — `att_horizon`,
  `horizon_vehicle_count` and `episode_reward` **all taken from the same source episode**, never mixed
  across fields — read from the committed `docs/data/p4_6_grid.json` (`behaviour@mappo1000`,
  `behaviour@random`) and **cross-checked bit-exactly** against `docs/data/p4_heldout_thresholds.json`
  and `/home/filip/rltraffic/output/p4_6/eval_random_behaviour.json`.
- **Cost: zero compute.** Both components are already measured on draws 1000–1099 at 5 seeds.
- **Paired by draw and A5-compliant by construction**: 500 records, the same (seed, draw) grid as every
  method cell.

**The three constraints, all implemented:**

1. **Labelled `constructed`, never `measured`.** The artifact carries `source: "constructed"`, the RNG
   seed, the realised per-seed draw assignment (the full list, not a digest of it), the expert fraction
   and the per-seed expert count. A reader can tell it apart from a rolled-out cell without reading code.
2. **It is a REALISATION, not an expectation.** `f × ATT_expert(d) + (1−f) × ATT_random(d)` would give
   the same mean and an **understated variance**, and a paired CI against an expectation would overstate
   precision. **The realisation is built.** A test asserts every constructed value is *equal to one of
   the two component values for that exact (seed, draw)* — which a per-draw average would fail.
3. **The bracket is reported beside it**: `mappo1000` **105.58203462874322** and `random`
   **428.8839383529867**, both re-read from the committed artifact in this session, so the reference
   is legible as an interpolation between two measured endpoints.

**Where it lives:** in the new module `offline/mixture_tiers.py`, **not** in
`method_tier_grid.BEHAVIOUR_REFERENCE_BY_TIER`. That keeps `tests/test_method_tier_grid.py:951`'s
assertion true and correct — *`method_tier_grid` declares no reference for a mixture; P4.7 supplies one

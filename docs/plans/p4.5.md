## 0. The one-paragraph summary

P4.4 **deduced** that %BC's top-10 % return filter performs *checkpoint selection*: 19 of its 20
streams come from MAPPO seeds 101 and 202, the two best behaviour checkpoints on the held-out pool.
This task **measures** it. Five arms, all trained and evaluated on P4.4's exact recipe and differing
only in which streams they see: `bc_top10` (20, top return — already measured, **re-used, not
re-rolled**), `bc_best2_20` (20 drawn at random from seeds 101+202), `bc_any_20` (20 drawn from all
five), **`bc_worst2_20`** (20 drawn from seeds 505+303, the two worst) and `bc_best2_all` (all 80 of
seeds 101+202, secondary). **The decisive comparison is `bc_best2_20` against `bc_any_20`** — same
size, same optimiser, same normalisation statistics, same budget, same draws, differing only in which
checkpoints produced the data — and **the fifth arm turns that contrast into an ordering**, which is
what makes a null result informative instead of weak (§2.4).

## 2. THE REGISTERED PREDICTIONS — copied verbatim from `BRIEF_13` §3, before the first gradient step

> **Primary:** `bc_best2_20` lands **within δ = 0.6263 of `bc_top10`** (103.1627). If matched-size
> random sampling from the two best checkpoints reproduces %BC, then the filter's contribution *is*
> checkpoint selection and the return ranking within those seeds adds nothing.
>
> **Secondary:** `bc_any_20` lands **worse than `bc_best2_20`** by an amount comparable to the
> behaviour-policy gap between the best two seeds and the five-seed mixture (**2.05 ATT**).
>
> ⚠️ **CORRECTED 2026-08-12 (§10.1, the implementer's finding, my error): 2.05 is the gap to the BEST
> SINGLE seed (202, 103.52859). The gap to the MEAN OF THE BEST TWO is 2.0134** (103.56864 against the
> mixture's 105.58203). Report both; score this secondary **directionally**, since it is a magnitude
> expectation and not a threshold.
>
> ⭐ **THIRD PREDICTION, added by §10.7 and to be copied verbatim into the plan file:**
> **`bc_best2_20` < `bc_any_20` < `bc_worst2_20` in ATT (lower is better) — monotone in
> behaviour-seed quality.** This is the actual mechanism claim: not *"does seed identity matter"* but
> *"does performance TRACK behaviour-mode quality"*. **If all three land together, seed identity does
> nothing and F3's reading collapses — which we would then learn decisively rather than from one null
> contrast.**

**What falsifies the primary, and what it would mean** (`BRIEF_13` §3, verbatim): *if `bc_top10`
beats `bc_best2_20` by more than δ, then ranking episodes within the good seeds does real work, F3's
reading is incomplete, and the paper must say the filter does **both** — a correction we would then
owe P4.4's packet.*

**δ is IMPORTED, not derived here.** A6's δ = 0.6263 is the DT's margin over its behaviour mixture,
re-used as this project's registered equivalence scale for this scenario — a choice stated here
rather than defended later. **The paired mean difference, its 95 % CI, the CI width and the
rank-biserial are therefore reported for every pair unconditionally**, so no reader depends on δ.

### 2.1 How each prediction is scored — ranked per `BRIEF_13` §10.5, fixed before the run

* **The registered forecast is scored by (A): `abs(mean_paired_difference) <= 0.6263`** — §3 says
  *"lands within δ of"*, which is a claim about a point estimate, and P4.4's `_forecast_outcomes`
  scored its forecast the same way.
* **An equivalence claim that may enter the paper requires (B): the whole 95 % CI inside `[-δ, +δ]`**
  — A6's rule, and the only one of the two that can support *"these are the same"* rather than
  *"we failed to find a difference"*.
* **Both are computed and both are reported.** If they disagree — mean inside δ, CI straddling — the
  declared name for that outcome is **"consistent with equivalence, not demonstrated at this
  power"**, reported with the CI width, and **it may not be written up as a match**.
* **Secondary: directional.** HELD iff the paired mean difference `bc_any_20 − bc_best2_20` is
  **positive** (worse) **and** its 95 % CI excludes 0. The magnitude is reported beside **both**
  reference gaps of §1.1; the brief gives no numeric threshold for "comparable" and I will not invent
  one after seeing the data.
* **Third (the ordering): HELD iff the three cell means satisfy `bc_best2_20 < bc_any_20 <
  bc_worst2_20` strictly.** Both adjacent paired differences are additionally reported with their
  CIs, and whether each CI excludes 0 is reported as the stronger statement — but the ordering itself
  is scored on the point estimates, so the criterion cannot be moved afterwards.

### 2.2 ⭐ WHAT A NULL MEANS NOW — `BRIEF_13` §11.2, verbatim, and it is why the fifth arm exists

> With four arms, `bc_best2_20 ≈ bc_any_20` was a **single null contrast** — weak, and fairly
> attributed to power. With `bc_worst2_20` present and a **3.8190 ATT** best-to-worst behaviour
> spread at six times δ, the *same* null becomes a **positive finding**: *seed identity in the
> training data does not transfer to the cloned policy's held-out performance, at a spread where it
> demonstrably could have.*
>
> **Declare it now, because afterwards it is unbuyable.** Without this sentence in the plan, a null
> gets written up as *"we found nothing"* when what was established is *"we ruled it out at a
> detectable spread"* — and nobody can tell the two apart from the artifact.
>
> ⚠️ **State it quantitatively, or it is rhetoric.** *"Could have detected it"* is a claim about
> power, so the packet must report the **`bc_best2_20` vs `bc_worst2_20` paired CI and its width**,
> and phrase the null as **"no effect larger than ±X found, against a 3.8190 ATT spread in the
> behaviour policies themselves"**.

**X is declared here so it cannot be chosen later:** `X = max(abs(ci95_low), abs(ci95_high))` of the
paired difference `bc_best2_20 − bc_any_20` — the largest effect the data leave standing, not the
half-width around a convenient centre. The power evidence quoted beside it is the **`bc_best2_20` vs
`bc_worst2_20`** paired CI and width, both reported whatever the outcome.

### 2.3 `bc_any_20` sits in the middle **in expectation**, not by construction — `BRIEF_13` §11.3

`bc_best2_20` and `bc_worst2_20` isolate behaviour modes **by construction**. `bc_any_20` is a
**mixture** of all five, so it sits near the five-seed mean **only in expectation over subset draws**;
the training-seed RNG makes that true across five draws rather than one. **Stated as an expectation,
never as a property of the design.**

**Predicted here rather than discovered afterwards:** `bc_any_20` should carry the **largest
subset-induced variance of the three matched-size random arms**, because its draw varies in *which
seeds appear* as well as in which streams. **If its five-seed spread is not the widest, that is worth
a sentence** — it would mean seed composition is not driving variance, which bears directly on §2.2's
null. The per-arm five-seed spread (max − min of the per-seed means) is reported for all three.

## 3. The five arms — declared in code, not in a shell command

| arm | n streams | pool | selector | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `bc_top10` | 20 | all 200 | top return | **already measured** — 500 episodes re-used |
| `bc_best2_20` | 20 | 80 streams of seeds **101+202** | uniform, no replacement | **new** |

| `bc_best2_all` | 80 | 80 streams of seeds 101+202 | all of them | **new**, secondary |

**Subset draws come from the training seed's RNG:** for training seed *s* the subset is drawn with
`numpy.random.default_rng(s)`. Each random arm therefore averages over **five different subsets**, so
its CI covers **subset variance and training variance**, which the P4.4 arms' CIs do not. Stated in
the packet and in the artifact.

**Everything else is P4.4's, unchanged:** 40,000 gradient steps, `TRAINING_SEEDS = (101, 202, 303,
404, 505)`, `BC_BATCH_WINDOWS = 64`, AdamW at `LEARNING_RATE`/`WEIGHT_DECAY`/`WARMUP_STEPS`/
`GRAD_CLIP`, `train_bc`, `evaluate_arm` over `HELD_OUT_DRAWS` (1000–1099), `engine_seed = 1000`,
`--torch-threads 1`.

**The normalisation statistics are the full training split's, for every arm.** The dataset is always
built over **all five** directories (`build_training_dataset`) and only then filtered by
`filter_stacked_to_streams`; no arm ever constructs a dataset over a subset of directories, because
that would refit `NormalizationStats` and make the arms incomparable. **Checked, not trusted:** the
`stats` block of all 20 new checkpoints must be byte-equal to that of P4.4's five `bc_top10`
checkpoints.

## 4. What is built

### 4.1 `offline/offline_baselines.py` — extended, not restructured

Additions only; **no existing function's behaviour changes except the two review fixes of §4.5.**

1. `streams_from_datasets(dataset, dataset_dirs) -> tuple[StreamReturn, ...]` — every stream whose
   `dataset_dir` is one of the given directories, in canonical order
   `(dataset_dir, episode_file, ix_id)`. Raises, naming **both** sides, if a requested directory
   yields no stream. Path comparison is `str(Path(x))` on both sides — normalising separators but
   **not** resolving symlinks, so a mismatch raises instead of silently matching.
2. `random_stream_subset(streams, count, rng) -> tuple[StreamReturn, ...]` — uniform sample without
   replacement, deterministic given `rng`. **The input is canonicalised *before* the draw**, so both
   the selected set and the output order are independent of the caller's ordering; the output is in
   canonical order. Raises, naming both numbers, if `count > len(streams)`; raises on `count < 1`.
3. `SELECTION_ARMS` — a frozen declaration of the four new arms (selector, behaviour seeds, count).
   The CLI takes `--stream-selector` and its parameters as `BRIEF_13` §4 requires **and refuses to
   run if they disagree with the declaration for `--selection-arm`** (approved, §10.6): a shell typo
   must not be able to redefine an arm.
4. `thread_regime() -> dict[str, Any]` (**§11.1**) — `OMP_NUM_THREADS`, `MKL_NUM_THREADS`,
   `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS`, `torch.get_num_threads()`, `torch.get_num_interop_threads()`, **read at
   call time**, never cached at import. Recorded **beside every timing this task reports**: each
   training run's `seconds`, each evaluation pass's wall time, and Gate B's.
   ⚠️ **`runtime_provenance()` is NOT modified** — it lives in the merged `offline/dt_gate.py`, which
   §7 freezes. The gap it leaves is the coordinator's `DEFERRED` 39/41, not mine to close.
5. CLI `train`, extended: `--stream-selector {top_return,datasets,random_subset}` (default
   `top_return` = **exactly P4.4's path, unchanged**), `--selector-seed` (repeatable behaviour
   seeds), `--subset-count`, `--selection-arm`. On the selection path one invocation trains **one arm
   across the five training seeds** and writes `docs/data/p4_5_selection.json`.
6. CLI `evaluate`: `--method` gains the four new arm names. The training artifact it reads is
   **derived from the arm name** (`p4_4_training.json` for P4.4's methods, `p4_5_selection.json` for
   P4.5's arms) rather than passed, so a P4.5 arm cannot be evaluated against P4.4's declaration.
7. CLI `report-selection` → `docs/data/p4_5_baselines.json`, via a new `selection_artifact(...)`. It
   reuses `_cell`, `paired_comparison`, `rank_biserial`, `mean_ci95`, `wilcoxon_signed_rank`
   unchanged: a second implementation of the protocol is how two arms stop being comparable.
8. `delta_verdict(mean_difference, ci95_half_width, delta)` — the same arithmetic as
   `equivalence_verdict` with **neutral** names (`within_delta`, `left_genuinely_better`,
   `right_genuinely_better`, `inconclusive_at_this_power`), because writing `dt_genuinely_better`
   into an artifact describing a BC-versus-BC pair would be a false label on disk — the defect class
   F1 was raised for (§10.6). A test asserts it agrees with `equivalence_verdict` under a documented
   name map over a grid of inputs: the double-compute.

**No second training function.** `train_bc` takes the filtered stack, exactly as `bc_top10` does.
**No agent change.** The architecture is identical across all arms.

## 10. Risks and residual conflicts

* **The two matched arms may be closer than the instrument can resolve.** P4.4's paired CI widths are
  ≈ 0.50–0.52 ATT on 100 draws. §2.2 is exactly the discipline that makes that outcome informative
  rather than empty — but only if `X` and the power contrast are reported, which §2.2 fixes now.
* **`bc_best2_20` and `bc_any_20` share a batch-index sequence.** `train_bc` draws batches from
  `default_rng(seed)` over `count` rows and all three matched arms have 7,200 rows, so for a given
  training seed the three runs visit the same row **indices** with different streams at them. This

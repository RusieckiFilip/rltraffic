## Resume behaviour

- **Training:** status is computed from disk on every call. A run is `done` (checkpoint + write-once record, the
  record's recipe this cell's, the checkpoint's canonical digest the record's) and skipped; `missing` and trained; or
  anything in between (a `.partial`, a checkpoint without its record or the reverse, another recipe, another digest)
  and REFUSED -- the whole `train` call stops before anything trains, and the file is left for a person to read.
- **Evaluation:** route (i) skips a cell whose complete 5 × 100 file is on disk; route (ii) and the old cells skip every
  episode file on disk (P8.4b's `run_campaign`); a route (ii) episode that does not reproduce route (i)'s `att_ours`
  stops the campaign (a refusal, never a skip); the manifests are written once and refused if they differ; P3a's
  records are written once.
- **Re-starting** a stage needs a new token. Everything finished is on disk and skipped; an episode in flight is rolled
  again; a training run in flight leaves its `.partial`, which is refused until a person removes it (it is a record of
  an interruption).
- **COMPLETE** is printed only when `status`, computed from disk, says `complete: true`.

## Deviations from the brief and the plan (each with its reason)

- **Evaluation no longer overlaps training (plan §6's calendar).** A cell is evaluated only after the LAST training run:
  a run in progress leaves its `.partial` on disk, and the status refuses a `.partial` by design (a half-written run is
  never skipped or retrained). An evaluation stage beside training would therefore have stopped at the first hz1x1
  run in flight. The plan's "evaluation overlapping as cells complete" is withdrawn; wall clock grows by the evaluation
  time (≈ 7 h at 6 workers by the plan's estimate). The driver's separate `evaluate` stage was removed before it was
  ever committed.
- **The evaluation's manifests are declared by one process (`declare-evaluation`) before the shards.** Each shard
  declaring its own could race another on the same write-once file.
- **The old hz1x1 cells are re-rolled in shards** (`old-cells --shard k --of N`), against their own manifest, refused
  without it.
- **A `d4rl` stage** in the same driver, under its own token: the IQL arm only, `--iql-target-dropout off`,
  `--compare-with docs/data/p8_3_d4rl_calibration.json`, the registered five seeds, outputs under
  `/home/filip/d4rl-calib-out/p9_1/` (refused unless empty).
- **The campaign keeps a Unix-time GPU trace and every run's record brackets its training** (`started_unix`,
  `finished_unix`); the report flags a run whose bracket spans a trace gap. Not in the brief; added because this
  pre-flight's own first `iql_m` run spanned a 10 h 45 min suspension of the machine and every trainer times itself
  with `time.time()`.
- **A27(c), P4.4:** the registration names "P4.4's BC-vs-IQL contrast"; P4.4 holds no BC-vs-IQL contrast. Its one
  statement in which IQL appears is `madt_vs_iql` (A6), which is re-scored with `iql_t` (Q8). The registration wins
  where the two differ; here it names nothing that exists, so the closest registered statement is used and named.
- **The fresh reference's arm name** is `behaviour@random_fresh` (M1 packet, already disclosed), not the plan's
  `random_fresh@random_fresh`.

| a write outside the work dir | `assert_writable` against 19 protected roots | all 15 `output/` siblings refused; pre-flight created nothing |

**The reproduction test is the acceptance test, and it is stronger than a hash by construction.** In
the negative control the wrongly-loaded checkpoint was a valid, intact file belonging to a different
seed — a sha256 would have passed it. A cell with no committed `att_ours` at any grain raises as
UNVERIFIED and is excluded from every verdict rather than reported with a caveat.

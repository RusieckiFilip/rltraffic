**Ordering.** Ours `iql > bc > bc_top10`; published (ranks only) `bc_top10 > iql > bc`. They
disagree — but **the half the brief's question rests on, `iql` above `bc`, reproduces the published
direction** (86.7 > 55.2).

**Why "no defect visible" and not "IQL is correct".** IQL's AWR diagnostics are healthy rather than
degenerate: clipped fraction 0.0483, near-zero 0.0498, effective sample size 0.0688, advantage std
1.2901 — collapsed neither to uniform nor to plain BC. Behaviour agreement 0.5975 sits **below**
`bc`'s 0.6533, i.e. it deviates from the behaviour policy, which is what an advantage-weighted
extraction should do. All arms reached the plateau diagnostic, so §6.3's extended-budget secondary
did not fire. **But the CI crosses zero**, so this is a failure to detect a defect, not a
demonstration of correctness.

trains on. Plan §3 A10 declares non-overlapping windows and nothing enforced it. I added
`test_bc_windows_tile_each_episode_without_overlapping` (every used row appears exactly once) and the

have found it.** M5 and M6 mutate files outside `calibration/`; both were applied to the working
tree, run, reverted, and `git status` was verified clean.

**Conflicts found between brief and repo** (repo wins)

   `ERROR: Package 'd4rl' requires a different Python: 3.12.13 not in '<3.11,>=3.7'` at commit

**Open questions / risks for the Master chat**
1. ⭐ **The reportable finding is the lost anchor.** D4RL's published locomotion scores are not
   reproducible under current MuJoCo (`mujoco-py` 2.1 → `mujoco` 3.11): one-step reward reproduces
   only to 5.6e-3, established with three independent checks. **Who writes this up, and where?** It
   bears on every 2026 paper still citing that table.
2. **The `iql` − `bc` CI crosses zero.** More seeds or more evaluation episodes would tighten it. Is
   the current strength sufficient for what P5.2 needs from it?
3. ⚠️ **Ruling 10c(b)'s premise is right about the environment and unproven about the adapter.** The
   Gate D bias is common to all three arms and cancels; **it does not follow that the codebook's cost
   is.** `bc_top10`'s collapse is exactly the shape an arm-specific adapter cost would take. I tested
   the one mechanism I could — and **falsified it** (the expert half quantises *better*: 0.4166 against
   0.4645, using 64/64 code words against 63/64). The remaining candidates (10,000 distinct windows
   against 100,000 at an identical budget, with `bc_top10` showing the *highest* behaviour agreement
   0.7207 while performing worst; and covariate shift over narrow expert *states*) are not separable
   here. **The `iql`-vs-`bc` contrast is the most insulated of the three** and is the one the question
   rests on.
4. **The single-Q deviation remains untested.** `train_iql` has no clipped double Q
   (`offline_baselines.py:1960-1963`), unlike published IQL, in every merged P4.x number. Registered
   before the run, not repaired — repairing it would invalidate P4.4–P4.7 and would be a method
   re-specification after seeing results. **A low score had two pre-declared explanations and this
   design controls only one.** Worth its own task before P5.2 leans harder on IQL.
5. **`BRIEF_23` collision risk:** if packaging moves to `find_namespace_packages`, `calibration/` would
   be swept into the wheel. It must stay out.
6. **Cleanup:** `/home/filip/d4rl-attempt-venv` (the failed `d4rl` install) can be deleted; I left it
   so the failure is inspectable.

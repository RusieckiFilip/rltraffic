## 9. Deviations, disclosures and defects found in my own work

### 9.1 A real defect in the campaign path, caught by the campaign and then by a new test

`_run_train` called `assert_declaration_matches_corpus(spec, dataset)` after that function's
signature changed to take the **selected streams** (finding A4). Every unit test passed, because none
of them called `_run_train`. The first real training job died on it in 90 seconds with
`AttributeError: 'dict' object has no attribute 'total_return'`.

**This is the third sighting of the shape `PROJECT_PLAN` section 7 now names a rule** — the helpers
are guarded, the function the numbers flow through is not. The fix was one line; the response is
`test_the_training_cli_path_runs_end_to_end_on_a_fixture`, which runs all four methods x five seeds
at a two-step budget through `_run_train` itself, and which **found a second defect on its first run**
(a stale `declared['split_streams']` key in a progress print).

### 9.2 The subsample RNG peek, restated under A4

During exploration I measured, for three candidate RNG seeds, the `random` tier's one-per-draw
subsample statistics — seed `20260813` -> max return -38369.0; seeds `1` and `42` -> -38733.0 — and
then declared `20260813`, the repo's date convention. **Under finding A4 this matters more than it
did under the plan's original rule**, because the DT's target is now the subsample's maximum.
Mitigating facts, all measured: `BRIEF_17` section 11 A1's own 2,000-draw simulation reports the
median outcome leaves the target unchanged; **no evaluation number existed for any subsample of any
tier when the seed was declared**; and the declared subsample's max, min, scale and IQL reward scale
turned out **identical to the full split's** (-38369.0 / -40294.0 / 40294.0 / 0.5194805194805194), so
A4 changed no declared number in this task at all.

### 9.3 One statistic still comes from the full split, and it is declared

`NormalizationStats` are fitted by `TrajectoryWindowDataset` at construction over **every** episode
it loads, so on `random` the state normalisation is fitted over 400 streams while training uses 200.
Changing that means editing a merged, reviewed loader or building a derived corpus directory, neither
of which is in scope. **No leakage is involved** — all 400 are training-split episodes of draws
1-200 — and the constant is shared by all four methods on that tier. Plan section 15.4 declares it;
the declaration artifact states it in `statistics_fitted_on`.

### 9.4 A2's shape: what I did and what I did not

The ruling says *"generalise the reporting path to a per-tier behaviour reference and a no-verdict
mode"* with a byte-identity regression gate. I did exactly that to `offline/offline_baselines.py`.
**P4.6's own grid artifact is written by the new module instead of by that path**, because
`p4.4-baselines/1.0` cannot express five tiers x four methods, 70 paired comparisons and three
scored predictions, and forcing it to would be a rewrite of a merged module rather than a
generalisation of it. The new module carries the per-tier behaviour references the ruling exists to
make possible and issues no verdict **by construction** (it imports no verdict symbol; a test asserts
that from the source text). **Flagged as a deviation in shape, not in substance, for the
coordinator's ruling.**

### 9.5 The "arrivals" figure could not be reproduced, and the reason is not in the repo

`BRIEF_17` section 6 quotes P4.5's volume check as **443.6 vs 446.1** arrivals. That number appears
in `docs/reviews/P4.4.md` and in `PROJECT_PLAN`'s Decisions Log, **in no code and in no artifact**;
it was computed ad hoc in the Master chat. My implementation — rebuild the collection's own
`FlowRandomizer` from the manifest and count `n_vehicles` per draw, verifying every draw against the
manifest's recorded `flow_draw_sha256` — gives **1814.9 vs 1820.9** on the same tier and the same
selection. The two are the same *sign* and the same *question*, in different units (their ratio is
4.09 and 4.08 respectively, which is suggestive and not an explanation).

**I could not reconstruct the original definition and I did not adopt either number silently.** My
definition is declared in plan section 4.3, is computable from the corpus alone, and its
reconstruction is hash-verified against the corpus's own record. **Open question 3 asks the
coordinator what the original statistic was.**

### 9.6 Check B is partly circular on one tier, by construction

P3's difficulty check ranks draws by the **MaxPressure** tier's per-draw ATT. On the `maxpressure`
tier itself, "the top decile by return" and "the easiest draws by MaxPressure ATT" are two functions
of the same episodes, so the measured **19/20 overlap (p = 2.2e-24)** is close to a tautology and
must not be read as evidence about selection behaviour. It is reported because the check is run
identically on every tier, and it is flagged here and in the artifact.

## 3. What each commit does

**C1.** `collect._alignment_for_door` returns `alignment_for_scenario_key(scenario_key)` — hz1x1's key resolves to
`declared_alignment()` itself, grid4x4's to its 16-id identity alignment. A SUMO draw's env is
`make_observer_sumo_env(..., halting_check=False)`, whose `_simulate` reads the teleport starts and the collisions
after every `simulationStep()`. `finish_sumo_draw_episode` records the engine reads and the per-second counts (with
their grain) in the draw's `sumo_draws` entry and raises before `finalize_episode()` on any teleport or collision, so a
refused episode writes nothing. `assert_logged_corpus_matches_probe_per_intersection` checks, in order: both committed
artifacts at their pins (calibration `3e9df8ee…`, zero-shot `c63c371f…`) before parsing; the manifest's format and
scenario key; disjointness from the calibration's own record; one episode per requested draw; each draw's door record
(counted every second, zero events, `cf_parity`, `time-to-teleport -1`, `engine_seed_drawn` 437485271 read from the
zero-shot artifact — Amendment A, Q14); per episode 360 decisions, the artifact's 16 ids, integrality, then `==` BY ID
through the episode's own `ix_ids`.

**C2.** The driver refuses, before its token and without writing, on: the implementer's tree, a module loaded from
another tree, a live run, not leading its process group, SIGINT ignored, RESCO's network or a parity config missing, a
dirty tree, **a non-empty corpus or run directory**, low memory, `corpus-preflight` failing, and either canary half.
After the token: `record-canary`, `collect-corpus` (one process), `SHA256SUMS` (atomic, re-verified, 101 lines), then
`corpus-gate` LAST, whose record is written only if it passes; `COMPLETE` or `FAILED`.

**C3.** `offline/few_shot.py`, the route of plan section 7, every step validated before any work:
1. The request: `init` is `source` or `scratch`, `k` is 5, 20 or 100, the budget and seed are integers, and the
   destination does not exist in a directory that does.
2. The source, at its pin (A20(a)), loaded weights-only with one allowlisted class. Its shape is checked (format,
   no mixing, 4 heads, the identity mask, normalised with statistics). So is P5.2's recorded recipe, field by field
   against the constants imported where `train_tier_dt` imports them: 40,000 steps, batch 64, lr 1e-4, weight decay
   1e-4, clip 0.25, warm-up 1,000, `dt_nomix_h4`.
3. The k targets from the digest-pinned calibration artifact, whose `rtg_scale` must equal the source's for every id.
   Each budget must carry `k`, the prefix `[201, 200 + k]`, Rule B, the mean, and its A24 role.
4. The prefix. `SHA256SUMS` is re-verified entry by entry, the directory holds exactly what it lists, the manifest is
   v1.1 in the source's scenario, and the draws are the whole band 201–300 once each. A missing draw is refused BY
   NAME; then the first k are taken. The prefix is checked disjoint from the source's training draws and the held-out
   pool.
5. The windows. `TrajectoryWindowDataset` is given the SOURCE's statistics object (and must adopt that very object),
   then `build_joint_index` in the SOURCE's node order and `stack_joint`. An episode whose id set differs is refused,
   never re-keyed by position.
6. `Utils.seed_everything(seed, seed_python_random=False)`, then the model from the PAYLOAD's config (`max_ep_len`
   360), and for the fine-tune only `load_state_dict(strict=True)`. That one call is the from-scratch switch.
7. Exactly B steps of `train_tier_dt`'s loop, reproduced rather than called: a fresh AdamW, `warmup_for(B)` with
   `lr_multiplier`, the clip, 64 joint instants per step with replacement from `np.random.default_rng(seed)`, the RTG
   divided per node by the frozen `rtg_scale`, and the frozen mask. The steps are counted, and the count is asserted
   before the write.
8. The payload: top-level `spatial-dt-checkpoint/1.0` (Amendment A1.1), the source's frozen parts exactly as read, the
   fine-tuned weights, the k targets, and provenance carrying `few_shot.format_version = "few-shot-checkpoint/1.0"`.
   There is no wall-clock value in it. It is serialised IN MEMORY and written once (fsync, then `os.link`, which
   refuses an existing file) — §8 item 5 says why.

The CLI's `train` runs one registered run on CUDA only, then writes the run's record (seconds, losses) once.

**C4.** The driver decides nothing in bash that could train a checkpoint twice, overwrite one, or let a fenced one
through; `offline.few_shot` does.

**`train` mode** (G6), all before the canary and the token:

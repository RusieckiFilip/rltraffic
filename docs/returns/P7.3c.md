## 3. What C1 and C2 do, in one paragraph each

**C1.** `collect._alignment_for_door` returns `alignment_for_scenario_key(scenario_key)` — hz1x1's key resolves to
`declared_alignment()` itself, grid4x4's to its 16-id identity alignment. A SUMO draw's env is
`make_observer_sumo_env(..., halting_check=False)`, whose `_simulate` reads the teleport starts and the collisions
after every `simulationStep()`. `finish_sumo_draw_episode` records the engine reads and the per-second counts (with
their grain) in the draw's `sumo_draws` entry and raises before `finalize_episode()` on any teleport or collision, so a
refused episode writes nothing. `assert_logged_corpus_matches_probe_per_intersection` checks, in order: both committed
artifacts at their pins (calibration `3e9df8ee…`, zero-shot `c63c371f…`) before parsing; the manifest's format and
scenario key; disjointness from the calibration's own record; one episode per requested draw; each draw's door record
(counted every second, zero events, `cf_parity`, `time-to-teleport -1`, `engine_seed_drawn` 437485271 read from the
zero-shot artifact — Amendment A, Q14); per episode 360 decisions, the artifact's 16 ids, integrality, then `==` BY ID
through the episode's own `ix_ids`.

**C2.** The driver refuses, before its token and without writing, on: the implementer's tree, a module loaded from
another tree, a live run, not leading its process group, SIGINT ignored, RESCO's network or a parity config missing, a
dirty tree, **a non-empty corpus or run directory**, low memory, `corpus-preflight` failing, and either canary half.
After the token: `record-canary`, `collect-corpus` (one process), `SHA256SUMS` (atomic, re-verified, 101 lines), then
`corpus-gate` LAST, whose record is written only if it passes; `COMPLETE` or `FAILED`.

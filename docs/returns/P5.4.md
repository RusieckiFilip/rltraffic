## 11. Limitations of what I shipped
- **The artifact is byte-reproducible at the same thread count, and not below it.** Measured, as the plan promised:
  `dt_spatial_h4` seed 101's F at 8 threads against 1 thread is **not** bit-identical (max abs diff 7.08e-09, max rel
  4.0e-07; r 0.0638782076532047 against 0.06387820764375952). **The support is identical** under both, so the defect
  check and every exact-zero claim are thread-independent, and the verdict is untouched. The artifact records
  `torch_num_threads: 8` in both the protocol and the runtime block.
- One tier, 200 windows, full windows only (the first 19 decisions of each episode are unrepresented), the last
  step's logits, a state-only substitution, a ratio of means. All are listed in the artifact's
  `what_this_does_not_say`.
- r is a **one-hop** statistic by registration; the 2- and 3-hop influence is reported per hop and enters no verdict.
- `pairs_within_same_episode` is 1 of 200: one drawn window's donor comes from its own episode. It is recorded, not
  excluded, because A22 says "another randomly chosen real window" and says nothing about episodes.

## The real discriminator — and the mechanism BRIEF_29 proposed is NOT the one

BL-1 item 2 asked me to state *"the mechanism (near-uniform logit shift)"*. **I measured it and it is
false, so I am not restating it.** Removing the common per-step shift from `fixedtime`'s logit delta
leaves **97.2 %** of it (`random`: 99.7 %). The shift is not near-uniform in either tier.

**The actual mechanism is softmax saturation, and the discriminator is the top-1/top-2 logit
margin.** Measured on seed 101, 5 streams, over all 11 interventions:

| tier | min top-1/top-2 margin | max per-step \|Δlogit\| | **min(margin / perturbation)** | can any grid target flip an action? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fixedtime` | 17.4137 | 1.0366 | **19.897** | no, with ~20× headroom |
| `random` | 0.2956 | 1.9240 | **4.874** | no, with ~5× headroom |
| `maxpressure` | 1.5122 | 3.6698 | 1.931 | no, but only just |
| `mappo1000` | 0.0929 | 6.7716 | **0.032** | yes |
| `mappo500` | 1.8747 | 7.4512 | 0.643 | yes |
| `mix33` | 0.0298 | 3.1827 | 0.351 | yes |

This predicts the flip-rate ordering exactly, which the "uniformity" account does not. It also
explains the four-orders-of-magnitude TVD gap at comparable logit movement: **the gap is in the
margin, not in the movement.** `fixedtime`'s median `p_max` is 0.999999998549, so `1 − p_max ≈ 1.45e−9`
— the TVD scale measured. `random`'s is 0.999999281392 → ~7.2e−7.

**BL-1 item 4 — does the reading `BRIEF_28` §8 item 2 fences survive on `fixedtime`?** On the measured
basis: the conjunction it requires is *flip rate 0 across the whole grid* (true, 55/55) *and TVD
negligible* (1e−9, which is negligible by any standard this project uses). **So the reading is
available for `fixedtime` — but on the margin headroom, not on an exact zero, and the two tiers that
flip nothing are 4× apart on that headroom (19.9× vs 4.9×), which `flip_rate = 0` alone conceals.**
Per BRIEF_29 §C I do not apply that word to the finding as a whole, and §8.2 below explains why the
whole-finding version of it is now positively contradicted.

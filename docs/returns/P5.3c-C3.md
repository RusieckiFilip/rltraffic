### The statistic (A26(d) as corrected by A26.1(c))
- **Two routes to the one-sided Wilcoxon, equal under `==`** on the statistic and p over 100 draws, in both directions:
  - route A, the module: zeros dropped, average ranks, the tie-corrected variance, the continuity correction, the repo's
    normal CDF;
  - route B, in `tests/test_p5_3c_statistic.py`: ranks by counting, `Var = Σr²/4`.

  The lower tail equals half the reviewed two-sided p whenever the statistic lies below its mean.
- **T1:** `s_d = Σ c_j·A_d(K_j)`, `c = (−2, −1, 0, +1, +2)`, over K ascending, left to right from zero; "less".
- **T2, T3:** "greater" on `(A_d(K) − A_d(20)) − δ` at K = 1, 2. `δ = 0.6263` is a literal, and a test asserts it equals
  `offline_baselines.DELTA_ATT` and `docs/data/p4_4_baselines.json`'s.
- **Holm:** step-down within {T1, T2, T3} at α = 0.05, ties in the family's order; then the partition (i) / (ii) / (iii).
- **The registered sentences, verbatim.** For (ii), `{NOT_SHOWN}` names the tests that did NOT reject. When exactly one of
  T2, T3 rejected, A26.1(c)'s clause (`K = {k} falls short … δ = 0.6263 s of ATT.`) goes right after the parenthesis
  `(K = 1: …; K = 2: …).`
- **The number format (Q16):** four decimals, U+2212 for the minus sign, the CI as `[low, high]`.

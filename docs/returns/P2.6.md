## 8. A false claim I published and then corrected

I wrote that the editable install's `sys.meta_path` finder "outranks every `sys.path` entry". **That
was false and I never measured it** — I inferred it from the `.pth` calling `finder.install()`.
Measured after the coordinator challenged it: the finder sits at `sys.meta_path[4]`, *after*
`PathFinder`; its `MAPPING` excludes `offline`; and `PYTHONPATH` does work from a non-repo cwd. The
real cause was plain `sys.path` ordering — under `python -c`, `sys.path[0]` is the cwd.

**Worse, found while checking that:** the `conftest.py` line I wrote to "remove that finder" filtered
on `type(f).__module__`, which those objects report as `builtins`. It removed **0 of 5** entries and
was inert for every run I reported. What actually worked was the `sys.path.insert(0, scratch)` beside
it.

**What does not change:** the 15 + 15 scratch result and the 379 after the patch landed. Every one of
those runs asserted `agent.MAPPOAgent.__file__` starts with the scratch prefix and would have aborted
otherwise — which is why a wrong theory could not become a wrong number. Corrected in both tracked
artifacts (`07b7797`) per "fix the class, not the sentence". **It also appears in commit `37569dc`'s
message, which is immutable.**

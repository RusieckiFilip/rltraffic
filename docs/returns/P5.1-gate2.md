## 4. Two findings the campaign produced that nobody predicted

### ⭐ 4.1 `%BC` collapsed to 749.58, and the mechanism is an exact set identity

The top-decile filter kept **320 of 3,200** streams — and they came from **8 of 16 intersections**,
with **8 contributing exactly zero**:

```
kept:  B2 84 · C2 77 · B3 66 · B1 32 · A1 22 · D2 19 · C1 16 · C0 4
zero:  A0 · A2 · A3 · B0 · C3 · D0 · D1 · D3
```

⚠️ **WITHDRAWN, 2026-08-18 (m3): the phrase *"exact set identity"* overstated this and is retracted.**
Measured here: the decile cut falls at **−114.0**, **302 of 320** kept streams are **strictly above**
it, and **32 streams tie at the cut** and fill the last **18** slots. **One of those ties belongs to
`B0`, the ninth node by own-max**, so a reverse tie-break yields **9** intersections, not 8. **The
mechanism is untouched — 302/320 from 8 nodes — and it is the transferable part.**
⚠️ **Also recorded, because it is a claim about a different tier that is false about this one:**
`offline/offline_baselines.py:418` says the P4 decile boundary is *"measured, and not a tie"*. **On
this tier it IS a tie, and nobody recorded that.**

**The 8 intersections kept are the 8 with the highest own-max return.** Per-intersection returns on a
network are dominated by **how much
traffic a node carries**, not by how well it was controlled (`C2` tops out at −64, `D1` at −142), so
a global return quantile is a **load sorter**. `bc_top10` was trained on the eight quietest
intersections and then asked to control all sixteen: **505 of ~1,300 vehicles are still in the
network at the horizon.**

> **The general form, and it is the most transferable thing in this packet: a return-quantile filter
> is a behaviour-quality filter only when returns are comparable across the units being filtered.
> On one intersection they are. On a network they are not.**

⚠️ **This is not a defect in P5.1's code.** It calls `offline_baselines.top_return_streams`
unmodified — the same function that produced P4.4/P4.5/P4.6/P4.7. **The method was applied
faithfully; the finding is about the method on a multi-intersection corpus.** A per-intersection
decile is a *different method* and I did not silently substitute one.
⚠️ **It also bounds §1b's R6** (*"%BC is almost indifferent to dilution"*), whose scope condition
reads "on THIS corpus". On this one it is catastrophic.

### ⭐ 4.2 The mixing arm fit its data **better** and controlled **worse**

Final training loss, from the campaign's own records:

```
dt_spatial  0.2058  0.2227  0.2103  0.1947  0.2034     (mean ~0.207)
dt_nomix    0.4412  0.4755  0.4310  0.4014  0.4360     (mean ~0.437)
```

⚠️ **Stated as measured, and the mechanism is NOT established:** I have training loss only, no
held-out validation loss, so I cannot separate over-fitting from compounding covariate shift under
closed-loop rollout. Both are consistent with this pattern; distinguishing them needs a measurement
nobody has run.

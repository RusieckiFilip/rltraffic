## 6. Deviations from the brief, each with its justification

1. **`report` takes a `cells=` parameter** the brief does not mention. Without it a unit test would
   need 4,700 chunks to satisfy the completeness check. The risk it creates (a caller narrowing the
   declaration) is closed mechanically: when `cells` is supplied, `report` **refuses to write into
   `docs/data/`**, and the artifact records `cell_set_source`. The campaign path passes nothing and
   gets `declared_cells(stage)`. Tested both ways.
2. **`anchor_choose` returns `(choose, diagnostics)`**, not a bare callable. I wrote the bare version
   first and it forced `decisions` to be taken from the declared horizon — which would have made
   `validate_cell_payload`'s `decisions == 360` check a tautology. Anchors now count their own
   decisions. (Mutant M11 exists because of this.)
3. **One canary per *stage*, not per campaign.** P7.2b's `report` refuses chunks carrying more than
   one `canary_seconds`. B1 runs two stages under two tokens and re-runs the canary at each start,
   so that rule would refuse every valid two-stage campaign. `report` records
   `chunk_canaries_by_stage` and refuses a chunk with **no** canary; the artifact states why it
   differs from P7.2b.
4. **`a17f` CLI added to `offline.transfer_curve`** rather than to `transfer_calibration`, where the
   gate function lives. The driver had nothing to call (`assert_logged_corpus_matches_probe` has no
   CLI). I put it in P7.3a's own entry point to avoid touching the parser P7.2b's driver depends on.

## 10. Limitations of what I shipped

1. **The pool has never been executed.** `run_stage`'s `spawn` `Pool` and `_worker` have no test that
   runs them: `spawn` re-imports the module, so a monkeypatched fake cannot reach a worker, and a
   real one costs a SUMO episode per cell. Tested without a simulator: the skip/resume decision (a
   complete work directory starts no worker) and `move_aside`. **First execution is F1's pre-flight
   pilot**, which is what F1 says the pilot is for. Until then, treat the pool as unproven.
2. **`run_cell` has never run end to end on a real episode.** T5 exercises its pieces on real SUMO
   (env factory, `dt_choose`, the real agent, the kwargs spy, the recorder, `att_env_from_info`) for
   20 decisions; the full-episode assembly is exercised only through fake seams. The pre-flight
   pilot closes this too.
3. **The `e_sumo`/`att_env` ρ agreement in the tests is fixture-constructed** (`e_sumo = att_env +
   20` on every cell). The tests prove the arithmetic and the pairing; they say nothing about what
   the two definitions will do on real data. That is the campaign's job.
4. **No stage-1 or final artifact exists.** `docs/data/p7_3a_zero_shot*.json` are not written.
5. **The driver header's schedule is the A6 harness pilot's**, not a pilot through this driver. The
   header states this in its own text and names what must replace it (F1's pilot, then F3's pad
   re-run if the canary moves more than 10 %).
6. `paired_comparison`'s Wilcoxon runs on **2 shared draws** in the tests. It does not crash and the
   block is well-formed; its statistical behaviour at n = 100 is untested here.

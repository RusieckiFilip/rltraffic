## 0. The result, first, before any interpretation

> ### It is checkpoint selection, and it is a dose-response: the cloned policy inherits the performance of the checkpoints that produced its data.
>
> Five arms, one corpus, one recipe, 100 registered held-out draws, five seeds, 40,000 steps. The
> three matched-size arms differ **only** in which behaviour checkpoints their 20 streams came from.
>
> | arm | streams | drawn from | `att_horizon` ± 95 % CI | n |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | `bc_top10` (%BC) | 20 | top return | **103.1627 ± 0.1701** | 500 *(re-used, not re-rolled)* |
> | **`bc_best2_20`** | 20 | random, seeds 101+202 | **103.5925 ± 0.1684** | 500 |
> | **`bc_any_20`** | 20 | random, all five seeds | **105.0458 ± 0.1993** | 500 |
> | **`bc_worst2_20`** | 20 | random, seeds 505+303 | **107.1209 ± 0.2188** | 500 |
> | `bc_best2_all` | 80 | all of seeds 101+202 | **103.2567 ± 0.1803** | 500 |
>
> **The ordering prediction held, monotone, with every adjacent CI excluding zero:**
> `103.5925 < 105.0458 < 107.1209`.
>
> | arm | source, **equal-weight** | difference | source, **at drawn composition** | difference |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | `bc_best2_20` | 103.5686 | +0.0239 | 103.5702 | **+0.0223** |
> | `bc_any_20` | 105.5820 | −0.5363 | **105.4237** | **−0.3780** |
> | `bc_worst2_20` | 107.3876 | −0.2667 | 107.4041 | **−0.2831** |
>
> ⚠️ **Corrected 2026-08-12 (review finding N11).** The equal-weight column compares each arm to a
> *uniform* mixture of its declared checkpoints; the five subsets it actually drew are not uniform
> (`bc_any_20` pooled `{101:15, 202:28, 303:15, 404:21, 505:21}` against 20 each). Against the
> mixture it **actually trained on**, `bc_any_20`'s gap is **−0.3780**, not −0.5363 — so ~30 % of
> that one arm's figure was sampling skew. **The two size-matched pure arms barely move**
> (+0.0239 → +0.0223, −0.2667 → −0.2831), which is why the ordering, the CIs and the conclusion are
> untouched. **This is precision, not a retraction.**
>
> **3.5284 of the behaviour policies' 3.8190 ATT spread transfers to the cloned policy — 92.39 %
> at equal weight and 92.03 % at drawn compositions** (drawn spread 3.8338).

**The plain sentence.** *On the `mappo1000` tier of `cf_hz1x1`, over the 100 registered held-out
draws, twenty trajectories drawn at random from the two best behaviour checkpoints produce a
behaviour-cloned policy at 103.59 ATT, while twenty drawn at random from all five produce 105.05 and
twenty from the two worst produce 107.12 — a monotone ordering in behaviour-checkpoint quality at
identical training-set size (7,200 windows), paired p = 1.7e−16 and 7.9e−17 on the adjacent
contrasts. The top-10 % return filter's advantage over plain BC is therefore substantially an effect
of **which checkpoints it selected**, measured rather than deduced.*

would have to be phrased and bounded. The contrast separated by **1.4532 ATT** (CI [1.2095, 1.6970]),
so that phrasing does not apply. The bound `X = 1.6970` is recorded in the artifact as declared, and
the power contrast (`bc_best2_20` vs `bc_worst2_20`, width 0.5861) is reported beside it whatever the
outcome, exactly as §11.2 required.

## 6. Deviations, findings and one failed prediction

### 6.1 ⚠️ §11.3's variance prediction FAILED, and it owes the sentence the brief asked for

Predicted: `bc_any_20` carries the largest subset-induced variance of the three matched arms.
Measured (max − min of the five per-seed means):

| arm | per-seed spread |
|---|---|
| `bc_best2_20` | 0.6115 |
| `bc_any_20` | **1.0419** |
| `bc_worst2_20` | **1.1933** ← widest |

**The prediction is wrong as stated.** `bc_any_20` is second, not first. The sentence the brief asked
for: **variance appears to track the arm's own performance level rather than the variety of its seed
composition** — the two worse arms have the wider spreads, and `bc_any_20`'s composition-driven
variety (its subsets differ in *which* seeds appear) still puts it well above `bc_best2_20`'s 0.6115.
So seed composition plausibly contributes, but it is not the dominant term. **Bearing on §11.2:** the
null did not occur, so this does not weaken a null — but it does mean the "widest spread" diagnostic
should not be used as a proxy for compositional variance in P4.6 without a better instrument.

### 6.3 The δ-rounding guard is entirely shadowed by the proximity guard

## 8. Limitations

1. **One tier, one scenario, one backend, one intersection, five behaviour checkpoints.** The
   dose-response is measured on `mappo1000`/`cf_hz1x1`/CityFlow. Nothing here says the top-decile
   filter selects checkpoints *in general* — P4.6 owns the other two instantiations.
2. **`bc_top10` is still 0.43 ATT better than `bc_best2_20`** (p = 4.3e−05). Checkpoint selection
   explains most of %BC's advantage, not all of it. `bc_best2_all` ≈ `bc_top10` **suggests** the
   residual is training-set size within the good checkpoints rather than within-seed return ranking,
   but the size-matched test of that hypothesis (a 20-stream *top-return-within-101+202* arm) was
   **not run** and is not in this task's fence.
3. **The three matched arms share a batch-index sequence** by construction (same `default_rng(seed)`,
   same 7,200 rows), so they are unusually well matched — a property, not a defect, but it means the
   arms are *not* independent draws of the training procedure.
4. **`bc_worst2_20` reaches ~0 training loss on ALL FIVE seeds** (0.00019325, 0.00000437,
   0.00000213, 0.00000179, 0.00000132), with `behaviour_agreement` **exactly 1.0 on four of five
   and 0.99997 on seed 202** — corrected from "several seeds" (review finding N10; the review's own
   "exactly 1.0000 on all five" is true only to 4 dp, and the fifth value is stated here).
   **This is the mechanism, not a defect: the arm imitates its behaviour policy perfectly, and that
   policy is the worse one.** Near-zero loss on a 7,200-window training set means the clone

## §6.1 — The RTG definition, with its alignment in one sentence

> `rtg[t] = Σ_{u=t..T-1} local_reward[u]` — undiscounted, **inclusive of `r_t`**, over that
> intersection's own `ix{i}_local_reward` stream, so `rtg[t]` pairs with **state row `t` and action
> row `t`**, and `rtg[T-1] == r_{T-1}` exactly.

Accumulated backwards in float64, stored float32. The loop is deliberately **not** `np.cumsum`: the
tests recompute with `np.cumsum`, and a check that shares its implementation with the thing it checks
is not a check. The accumulation *order* is identical, so the comparison is `==` and never a
tolerance. A third route (`math.fsum`, exactly rounded) cross-checks it, and asserts its
integer-reward precondition rather than assuming it.

## `avail_mask` — a claim constraint for P4/P5, under its own heading

**Full population, not a sample:** all **4800 episodes / 32 000 intersection streams**:

```
episodes=4800  intersection-streams=32000
all-True masks: 32000/32000
masks excluding the taken action: 0
tiers with a binding mask: none
```

Mechanism verified in source: the corpus was collected with `control_mode: acyclic`, and
`envs/phase_control.py:583` `AcyclicPhases.available_actions()` returns every green phase.

**Consequences P4/P5 inherit:**
1. **Action masking never binds anywhere in our data.** A no-mask ablation on this corpus would
   measure nothing — it would compare a model against itself.
2. **The paper must not credit masked-action modelling as a learned capability.** Masking stays in
   the model (the env raises on illegal actions; masks bind under cyclic modes and P6 lane closures),
   but on this corpus it is inert.
3. "The mask never excludes the taken action" is **true by construction** here, so asserting it is a
   tautology. The corpus test therefore asserts the falsifiable population claim — *every stored mask
   is all-True* — which fails the day a corpus is collected under a cyclic mode, exactly when a
   consumer needs to know. The loader's mask handling is tested against a fixture where the mask
   genuinely binds (odd rows offer only the taken action).

## What the next task (P4) may assume about this one

- **`att_per_step` is float32**: cast a float64 horizon value down with `np.float32()` before `==`.

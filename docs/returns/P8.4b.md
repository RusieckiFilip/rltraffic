## 3. ⭐ The `fixedtime` collapse — a scientific finding

On grid4x4's `fixedtime` tier, **4 of 7 arms carry distinct per-episode ATT vectors**:

- `behaviour@fixedtime` ≡ `dt_nomix@fixedtime` ≡ `dt_spatial@fixedtime`
- `bc@fixedtime` ≡ `bc_top10_perix@fixedtime`

**It is confined to `fixedtime`.** Re-measured over the full 100-draw × 5-seed vectors:
`mappo1000` 6/6 distinct, `maxpressure` 7/7, `random` 6/6.

Two consequences, and they point in opposite directions:

1. **It is evidence the pipeline works.** Cloning a deterministic clock policy returns the clock;
   `dt_nomix` and `dt_spatial` reproducing `behaviour` exactly on a fixed-time tier is what a correct
   imitation pipeline should do on a deterministic teacher.
2. **It removes all discriminating power from P5.2's `fixedtime` rung.** P5.1's P2a contrast is
   `mean(ATT_spatial − ATT_nomix)`, which on that rung is **identically zero by construction, not by
   measurement**.

`bc_top10_perix` were all identical. Over the full vectors `bc_top10` separates; only `bc` and
`bc_top10_perix` coincide. The three-draw read was too small and the full-vector measurement
supersedes it.

## 4. The reproduction acceptance test, and its arm-dependent power

Every re-derived cell must reproduce its committed `att_ours` **exactly**; a cell with no committed
value at any grain is UNVERIFIED and excluded from every verdict. A checkpoint sha256 proves a file
is intact, not that it is the right file for that arm — and the episode is rolled anyway, so the
stronger check is also the free one. It caught two real failures.

⚠️ **Its power is ARM-DEPENDENT, and this belongs beside the test rather than in a footnote.**
`grid4x4/bc@fixedtime` is **seed-invariant**: all five seeds produce bit-identical committed ATT on
every draw although the five checkpoint files are md5-distinct. A seed mix-up there is
**undetectable — and harmless**, because the numbers are identical. Within the collapsed groups of
§3 the same holds across arms. The test remains correct; it cannot discriminate where the arms
themselves do not.

## 1. The defect and its correction, in one paragraph

P5.2 (`BRIEF_27`) trained BC, %BC and IQL on grid4x4 at four data tiers on its declared 200 episodes per tier. At the random
tier — the only one with more episodes available, 400 — `_run_train_baselines` filtered BC's and %BC's batches to the declared
streams but built IQL's transition table from the WHOLE dataset (`offline/tier_sweep.py:1820` at `9460800`, the commit every
original checkpoint records; `:2078` at `f077875`), so IQL trained on 2,304,000 transitions while every other arm trained on the
declared 1,152,000; the checkpoints' `training_streams: 3200` (the selected streams) hid it. P5.2b fixed the source
(`tier_sweep.iql_transition_table`: `method_tier_grid`'s two helpers composed, refusing unless the table holds exactly the
declared streams' transitions, by identity and by count), re-trained the five IQL seeds on the declared 1,152,000 rows under the
originals' thirteen hyperparameters (enforced, not copied) in P5.2's default CUDA regime, evaluated the corrected cell by P5.2's
own path (`att_ours`) and by P8.4b's own cell runner (`att_ours`, `att_engine` and the admission companions) on the same 500
episodes, and recomputed every random-tier statement that involves IQL, before and after, under both definitions. **The corrected
IQL is ≈ 7.5 s slower under both definitions, and no registered statement's outcome changes** (Amendment C, C1). The original
cell is kept, unchanged, as the record of the defect.

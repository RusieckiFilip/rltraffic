## 3. A coordinator error, recorded because the standard is the same for mine

My first verification pass asserted `episode_reward == sum(reward_series)` and reported **500/500 failures**.
The code is right and the assertion was mine to begin with: `run_cell`'s `choose` records
`info["intersections"][ix]["reward"]` **before** calling `agent.act`, and by contract C6 the `info` in hand at
decision *t* describes the state after step *t−1*. The series is therefore **shifted by one by design** —
`offline/transfer_calibration.py::rtg_advanced_every_decision` documents exactly this under Amendment D1,
including that an earlier version of the check got the direction wrong and recorded `False` for a working
mechanism. Consequences, all falsified rather than argued: `reward_series[0]` is `-0.0` on **500/500** cells
here and on a 200-cell sample of P7.3a's 4,000; the residual `episode_reward − sum(reward_series)` is one
reward-sized quantity (−129…−69 here, −301…−14 in P7.3a) and is the final step's reward, which the series
never holds; and the RTG identity above holds bitwise on both tasks. **No defect. The lesson is the project's
own: I verified the artifact against a relation I had assumed rather than against the one it documents.**

## 6. The finding that matters for the paper

**The C3 curve's k = 200 endpoint sits FAR BELOW its zero-shot start, and the comparison is exact** (§4):

| point | ρ (`e_sumo`) | ATT `e_sumo` | entered / created | pending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| zero-shot `mappo1000 / b_mean_k100` *(P7.3a, 2026-09-18)* | **+1.8895** | 141.35 | 1813.7 / 1814.3 | 0.7 |
| zero-shot `mix50 / b_mean_k100` *(P7.3a, 2026-09-18)* | **+1.7264** | 180.99 | 1761.8 / 1814.3 | 52.6 |
| **full-retrain anchor `anchor_k200`** *(this campaign)* | **+0.9830** | 361.86 | 1714.8 / 1814.3 | 99.5 |

**It is not a measurement artefact.** The obvious alternative explanation — that the zero-shot model flatters
its ATT by holding vehicles out of the network, the boundary-queueing mechanism this project already measured
at P5.3b — is refuted by the throughput column: `mappo1000/b_mean_k100` admits **1813.7 of 1814.3** created
vehicles with **0.7** pending, better than MaxPressure and better than the anchor. It dominates on delay *and*
on throughput.

**The cause is the demonstration data, not the dynamics.** The anchor is upper-bounded by the policy that
generated its corpus, and that policy is MaxPressure; the zero-shot subjects were trained on CityFlow MAPPO
corpora, a better behaviour policy. ρ measures how much of the fixed-time → MaxPressure gap an arm closes, so
an arm cloning MaxPressure is pinned near 1.0 **by construction**. This is C1 — the data-quality ladder —
reappearing as the dominant effect inside C3.

**Three consequences, none of which is a defect and all of which must be written down before they are
discovered by a referee.**

1. **The "full retrain" point is not a ceiling on achievable SUMO performance, and the pre-registration
   already says so.** A18(a), registered 2026-09-15 **before any target-domain number existed**, reads: *"It
   is the curve's k = 200 endpoint and the paper says exactly what it is: what target-domain probe data alone
   buys the same architecture — not an upper bound on achievable SUMO performance, and not a target-domain
   online policy."* A18(b) registers why no online SUMO anchor exists (no MAPPO-on-SUMO path, contract C8's
   metric-set defect, ≥ 1,000 on-policy episodes per seed) and defers it to P11 **as a limitation named in the
   paper's C3 section, not in a footnote**. The artifact carries both sentences in
   `what_this_does_not_say`. Nothing here needs re-running; it needs to be *led with*.
2. **H3's third clause is void as registered, because its second was refuted.** H3 reads *"zero-shot transfer
   is positive but incomplete — better than fixed-time, worse than within-backend MaxPressure — and closes
   substantially by k = 100."* P7.3a refuted *worse than MaxPressure* (ρ = 1.89 / 1.73). There is therefore no
   gap for k = 100 to close, and clause 3 cannot be evaluated in the direction it was written. The honest
   statement is that the clause is **void, not failed** — it presupposes a sign the data does not have.
3. **P7.3c, if it runs, measures interference, not gap-closing.** Its k ∈ {5, 20, 100} fine-tunes the *source*
   model on the *same MaxPressure SUMO probe data* the anchor was trained on. With the two endpoints now
   measured — 1.89 zero-shot, 0.98 at k = 200 — the expected shape is **monotone degradation**: fine-tuning on
   weaker demonstrations erodes a strong transferred policy. That is a legitimate and interesting result, and
   it is a different experiment from the one H3 describes. **It is deferred, which means the framing can be
   fixed before any number exists — the only moment at which fixing it is free.**

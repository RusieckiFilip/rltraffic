## Cell 3 — Reason

The P7.3d merge review (`docs/reviews/P7.3d.md`, A-2) found grid4x4 to be the one scenario on which H3's third clause is
testable at all. Testing it without a written threshold would leave *"substantially"* to be read off the number. Every degree
of freedom fixed here is one a referee would otherwise suspect of being chosen after the curve: the threshold, the estimator,
the zero-shot reference series and its licence, the reading of the confounded interior points, the ablation's reduction and
the absent anchor. **Half the gap is chosen for FORM** (a fraction of the gap, invariant to the gap's size) **and not for any
outcome, because no few-shot number exists on any scenario.** **Stated limits:** one scenario, one subject, one engine seed
(A18(c)). The demonstrator is MaxPressure, so closure is toward MaxPressure's level by construction of the data, which is
exactly what clause 3 asks and nothing more. The criterion is about `E_sumo`.

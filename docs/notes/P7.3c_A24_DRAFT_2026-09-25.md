## Cell 3 — Reason

The P7.3d merge review (A-2) found grid4x4 to be the one scenario on which H3's third clause is testable at all. Testing it
without a written threshold would leave *"substantially"* to be read off the number. Every degree of freedom fixed here is
one a referee would otherwise suspect of being chosen after the curve: the threshold and its boundaries, the estimator and
the pairing, the reference series and the condition that licenses it, the treatment of an incomplete campaign, the
once-only trainings, the reading of the prompt-confounded interior points, the attribution to transfer, the budget's
sensitivity, and the absent anchor. The design was reviewed once before registration (`docs/reviews/A24-proposal.md`);
three of its blocking findings changed the text, and three major ones — the prompt control, the attribution control and the
unreduced secondary — changed the design. **Half the gap is chosen for FORM** (a fraction of the gap, invariant to the gap's
size) **and not for any outcome, because no few-shot number exists on any scenario.** **Stated limits:** one scenario, one
subject, one engine seed (A18(c)); the demonstrator is MaxPressure, so closure is toward MaxPressure's level by construction
of the data, which is exactly what clause 3 asks and nothing more; the criterion is about `E_sumo`; the fine-tunes are not
bit-reproducible on this GPU unless the measurement of (b) shows otherwise, which is why they run once and are pinned.

### [x] S1-5 — what a warm-start fine-tune needs that does NOT exist today (minimal, tied to code)  (appended 2026-09-25T15:40 by `date`)

Needed: a path that rebuilds `SpatialDecisionTransformer` from the checkpoint's saved `config` (n_nodes 16, n_head 4, spatial_mixing False, context 20, max_ep_len) and loads `payload["model"]` — rather than deriving config from the new data.

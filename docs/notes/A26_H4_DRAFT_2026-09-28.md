## Cell 3 — Reason

H4 is the paper's answer to the controversy it opens with — DataLight's *DT cannot be applied to TSC* was measured at K ∈ {1, 2}
with one hardcoded prompt — and §1 registered it as confirmatory so that the answer *cannot be assembled after the fact*. Every
degree of freedom a referee would suspect is fixed here: the contrast (ranks, one direction), the family and its correction, the
margin (A6's, not a new one, with its scale transfer cited), the budget (fixed, with the supervision asymmetry measured by a
registered secondary), no reuse of the published checkpoints as an arm, the metric, and what K = 1 is. **The expectation is
registered against the hypothesis** because the data already on `main` say context is unlikely to matter on expert-like data; a
sweep that found outcome (ii) on `mappo1000` and a material gap on `mix50` would be the fair statement of DataLight's finding: it
depends on the data the model is given. **Stated limits:** one scenario; the confirmatory decision on one corpus, the registered
one; a fixed budget, with its sensitivity checked at K ∈ {1, 2} only; δ's transfer of scale is a stated choice, co-reported on both.

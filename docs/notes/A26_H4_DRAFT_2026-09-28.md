## Cell 2 — Change

**A26 — H4's CONTEXT-LENGTH SWEEP IS DECLARED: the subject, the five arms, the training rule, the evaluation, the ordered-levels
statistic, the meaning of *materially below the plateau* as a number, the decision rule and its multiplicity — all fixed while the only
context length ever trained in this repository is K = 20.**

**(a) THE SUBJECT AND THE SCENARIO — P4's, unchanged.** The P4 validation scenario (§1–§2): `cityflow1x1` (hangzhou 1×1), the
`mappo1000` corpus (`datasets_v11/cf_hz1x1__mappo1000__seed{101…505}`), training draws 1–200, the held-out pool 1000–1099 (100 draws),
one episode per (seed, draw) on CityFlow with the env seed rule of §5 (the engine seed is inert; demand is the draw). **The recipe is
P4's, read from its five checkpoints' provenance:** the one-head `DecisionTransformer` — 3 layers, 1 head, `d_model` 128, dropout 0.1,
`max_ep_len` 360; batch 64, AdamW lr 1e-4, weight decay 1e-4, warm-up 1,000, gradient clip 0.25; states normalised on the training
split; the naive in-domain prompt (`target_rtg` = the maximum training-episode return, −5762; `rtg_scale` = the largest |RTG|, 9991 —
properties of the corpus, identical for every K); greedy action selection. **The training budget is FIXED at 40,000 gradient steps
for every K** — P4's final count (its declared 20,000 was raised once under P4's plateau rule; the raise rule is NOT re-run per K,
because a K-dependent budget would confound context with training). Seeds 101, 202, 303, 404, 505 at every K.

**(b) THE FIVE ARMS AND WHY THEY ARE COMPARABLE.** K ∈ {1, 2, 5, 10, 20}, five seeds each: **twenty-five trainings, all fresh, at one
commit, in one campaign** — P4's K = 20 checkpoints are NOT reused as an arm. **The K = 20 reproduction gate:** the five retrained
K = 20 models must equal P4's five checkpoints TENSOR FOR TENSOR (`torch.equal` on every parameter; P4's files lack a later payload
field, so byte identity is not the test), which proves the sweep's trainer IS P4's; a difference stops the sweep before any other K
is evaluated and is a finding, not a number. **Why the arms differ only in context:** the window sampler's index is K-invariant
(`offline/dataset.py:647-651`; the survey's H2), so under a given seed every K trains on the identical stream of sampled windows, each
carrying its first K steps of context. **K = 1 is the registered degenerate anchor** (§1); in this code a K = 1 model conditions on the
current state, the current return-to-go token and the absolute timestep embedding — a one-step return-conditioned policy, not pure
behaviour cloning — and the paper describes it so. The evaluation rollout keeps a rolling window of exactly K steps read from the
checkpoint's config (`agent/DTAgent.py:618-660, 746-763, 860-887`), with an explicit K = 1 branch; no test exercises the agent at
K = 1 or 2 today — `BRIEF_42` adds them, red first.

**(c) THE EVALUATION.** Each of the 25 models on the 100 held-out draws, one episode each, greedy, the naive prompt: **2,500 cells**.
The metric is A11/A15's primary, **`att_engine`** (the pool-clock, all-created ATT), with `att_ours` (P4's `att_horizon`) co-reported —
P4's own rows carried `att_ours` only; P5.3b and P8.4b re-derived the same five K = 20 checkpoints under `att_engine` (mean 100.7032),
and the sweep's K = 20 arm must reproduce those re-derived rows under `==` on every draw (a second reproduction gate, on the
evaluation path). **The per-draw unit** (§2, §8): for each K and draw d, the mean over the five seeds of that episode's ATT, `A_d(K)`.

**(d) THE STATISTIC AND THE DECISION RULE — the number this row exists to fix.** **The trend:** for each draw d, the linear-trend
contrast over the five ORDERED levels, `s_d = Σ_j c_j · A_d(K_j)` with K ordered (1, 2, 5, 10, 20) and the equally spaced contrast
`c = (−2, −1, 0, +1, +2)` on the level's rank (ranks, not log K, so that no spacing choice is made after the fact); *performance
improves with K* means ATT falls with K, i.e. **s_d < 0**. **The confirmatory test T1:** §8's Wilcoxon signed-rank on {s_d}, n = 100
draws, ONE-SIDED (s < 0), α = 0.05; the effect size is the mean s_d with its 95% CI, and the paired differences A_d(K) − A_d(20) for
each K with CIs. **The plateau clause, as two further confirmatory tests:** the plateau level is K = 20's (the largest registered
level, P4's subject); *materially below* is a paired ATT margin of **δ = 0.6263** — A6's registered equivalence margin, the only
margin this registration carries — applied on the primary `att_engine` (a stated choice: A6 fixed δ on P4.4's `att_ours` scale;
between-arm differences are of the same magnitude on both definitions, and the `att_ours` computation is co-reported). **T2:**
Wilcoxon one-sided on {A_d(1) − A_d(20) − δ} > 0; **T3:** the same for K = 2. **H4's family for Holm–Bonferroni (§8) is {T1, T2, T3}, α =
0.05; H4 HOLDS if and only if all three reject after Holm.** Any other reading — T1 alone, or the point estimates against δ — is
reported as exploratory and does not decide. **Reported beside, never deciding:** every pairwise K contrast with CIs; the same
computations on `att_ours`; the per-seed s (five values, SD ddof 1); where the curve stops improving (the smallest K whose paired
difference from K = 20 is within δ), as a description of the plateau. **Registered expectation, written before any point exists:**
monotone improvement from K = 1 to K = 20 with the largest step between K = 2 and K = 5 — refuted by any adjacent-level paired
difference whose CI lies on the wrong side, reported as a refutation of this expectation, not folded into the decision.

**(e) §10's outcome rows for H4 stand unchanged** (*H4 holds → DataLight's negative result is explained as a configuration artifact of

## Cell 3 — Reason

H4 is the paper's answer to the controversy it opens with — DataLight's *DT cannot be applied to TSC* was measured at K ∈ {1, 2}
with one hardcoded prompt — and §1 registered it as confirmatory *precisely so that our answer … cannot be assembled after the fact*.
Every degree of freedom a referee would suspect is fixed here: the contrast (ranks, one direction), the family and its correction,
the margin (A6's, not a new one), the budget rule (fixed, not per-K), the reuse question (no reuse; a reproduction gate instead), the
metric (the registered primary, with P4's own co-reported), and what K = 1 is. **Stated limits:** one scenario and one corpus (the P4
validation scenario, as registered); a fixed budget of 40,000 steps for every K, so a K = 1 model that would need more steps is not
given them — the paper says so; δ's transfer from `att_ours` to `att_engine` is a stated choice, co-reported on both.

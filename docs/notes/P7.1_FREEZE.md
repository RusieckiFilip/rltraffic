## §"Metric"

### 1. The definition pair ρ is computed under — and the measurement that decides it

**Proposal: ρ is computed on the POOL-CLOCK, ALL-CREATED pair on both backends** — `att_engine` on
CityFlow (already primary on hz1x1 under Rule R) and **`E_sumo`** on SUMO — with the
ADMITTED-population pair (`att_ours`; the SUMO env's `average_travel_time`) **reported beside it in
every table**, extending A11(b)'s unconditional-disclosure discipline to SUMO.

**The reason is a measurement, not an argument about fairness.** `PREREGISTRATION` §3.4 normalises
*within* each backend precisely so that raw travel times never cross the boundary. That removes the
incomparability of LEVELS. It does not remove an incomparability of DEFINITIONS, and on 2026-08-31
the coordinator registered the threat that CityFlow has two ATT definitions and SUMO one, so
`ρ_cityflow` is definition-dependent while `ρ_sumo` is not. **Half A built SUMO's missing twin and
measured the consequence** (means of 5 episodes per arm; `fixedtime = 0`, `maxpressure = 1`):

| definition | Δ CityFlow | Δ SUMO | ratio | ρ_random CityFlow | ρ_random SUMO | gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **admitted** (`att_ours`, SUMO env) | 37.6645 | 11.6050 | **3.246×** | −3.6154 | −9.6728 | **6.0574** |
| **pool-clock** (`att_engine`, `E_sumo`) | 235.1994 | 237.6252 | **1.010×** | −1.4750 | −1.1140 | **0.3610** |

⭐ **Under the admitted definitions the two backends' anchor spreads differ by 3.2× and ρ_random by
6.06. Under the pool-clock pair the spreads agree to within 1 % and ρ_random to within 0.36.** The
admitted pair is not measuring the same quantity on the two backends: CityFlow's admitted metric
excludes vehicles the insertion buffer never released, SUMO's excludes a *different* set on a
*different* clock, and the residue lands in the denominator of ρ. The pool-clock pair asks both
engines the same question — *how long did every vehicle the demand created spend waiting or
travelling, counted from when the demand created it* — and the two answers agree.

⚠️ **What this does NOT say.** It does not say the two engines behave alike; Δ agreeing to 1 % is a
statement about the ANCHOR SPREAD, not about the levels, which differ by 190–240 s. It does not
re-open P7.0's branch (§4). And it is one scenario, one intersection, nominal demand, 5 episodes per
arm.

### 2. The frozen SUMO regime: teleporting disabled

**Proposal: every later SUMO measurement runs the `noteleport` configuration** —
`scenarios/hangzhou_1x1_bc-tyc_18041610_1h_parity/hangzhou_1x1_bc-tyc_18041610_1h_parity_noteleport.sumocfg`,

`.sumocfg` sets it; CityFlow never teleports. **So P7.0's SUMO cells, and half A's A1, ran with
teleporting ENABLED and must be labelled so wherever they are reported.**

### 7. The frozen backend-neutral feature set

**Proposal: three features, in this order, and nothing else.**

1. `lane_vehicle_count` over the canonical incoming lanes,
2. `lane_waiting` over the same lanes, in the same order,
3. a **9-wide green-action one-hot** in CityFlow's file-phase convention.

**Canonical order = the CityFlow corpus's own per-intersection `incoming_lanes` discovery order**
(`utils/cityflow_utils.py:95-110`), because the trained models read that order and the corpus cannot
move. On hz1x1 that is, with each lane's movement set and its SUMO partner:

| position | canonical lane | movements | SUMO partner | SUMO position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `road_0_1_0_1` | s | `road_0_1_0_0` | 0 |
| 1 | `road_0_1_0_0` | l | `road_0_1_0_1` | 1 |
| 2 | `road_1_0_1_1` | s | `road_1_0_1_0` | 2 |
| 3 | `road_1_0_1_0` | l | `road_1_0_1_1` | 3 |
| 4 | `road_2_1_2_1` | s | `road_2_1_2_0` | 6 |
| 5 | `road_2_1_2_0` | l | `road_2_1_2_1` | 7 |
| 6 | `road_1_2_3_0` | l | `road_1_2_3_1` | 5 |
| 7 | `road_1_2_3_1` | s | `road_1_2_3_0` | 4 |

State width **25** on CityFlow (8 + 8 + 9) against **32** on SUMO (8 + 8 + 16). **8 of 8 lane ids
denote a different physical lane in the two files**, and on hz4x4 gudang **128 of 192**. Sorting
fixes the order and leaves the denotation wrong; the pairing is by MOVEMENT.

**The phase map**: green action `k` → CityFlow file phase `k+1` → SUMO file phase `2k`; SUMO's odd
phases map to CityFlow's clearance slot 0. The slot is **dead in the corpus** — 577,600 `current_phase`
rows across all 1,600 hz1x1 episodes carry 1–8 and none carries 0 — and is kept only so the frozen
width does not move. `transfer_gate.green_action_lane_sets` agrees **8/8** under the correspondence
and **4/8** under the identity, the four being exactly the actions that release both lanes of one
road and are therefore symmetric under a within-road swap.

**Excluded by name**: `drq_norm` (RESCO's `(1, n_lanes, 5)` structured observation — backend-specific
by construction and it defines the whole observation), and every SUMO- or MOSS-only metric feature.
`metric_keys` is **empty by default** and must be named explicitly by a caller, because C8 makes the
metric set part of a MAPPO checkpoint's MDP: `SumoEnv` unions `average_travel_time` into its set and
CityFlow does not, so a CityFlow-trained checkpoint raises on SUMO (`global_feature_dim` 3 vs 4). The
DT reads per-intersection `state` only and is unaffected.

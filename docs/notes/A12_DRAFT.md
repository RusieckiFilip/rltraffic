## Working notes — not part of the registered row

**Why (3c) is required but not gating.** Making it gating would let a surprise about `metrics/cityflow.py`
block a question about `att_engine`. Making it optional would mean it never happens. Required-and-reported
is the only combination that gets the decomposition measured without coupling two independent questions.

**Why the cadence term is larger on grid4x4 (7.07) than hz1x1 (4.19).** Not established, and not claimed.
A plausible mechanism is more completions per decision window on a 16-intersection network, so the
window-end detection bias is applied to more vehicles — but that is a hypothesis, and (3c) is what would
measure it. **It is recorded as an open question, not as a finding.** T1's M1 estimated the effect at
+4.99 / +4.89 / +4.72 s on hz1x1 only; the grid4x4 figure is outside that range and nobody has explained it.

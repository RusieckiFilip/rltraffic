# P7.3 — the few-shot half, costed on measured bases (2026-09-14)

**Why this note exists.** The zero-shot arm's cost (≈ 11–13 h sequential) was on record; the few-shot
half — fine-tuning the DT on `k ∈ {5, 20, 100}` SUMO episodes for two subjects × five seeds, evaluating
each, and the full-retrain anchor — had never been costed. The author asked for the number **before**
`BRIEF_37`, with its measured basis, because this project revised a schedule from an unmeasured rate
three times in the week of 2026-09-12. Every rate below names the run that produced it; nothing is
extrapolated from a different simulator or a different machine state.

| **(e)** anchors on the held-out SUMO pool: fixed-time, MaxPressure, random (random × 5 seeds) | 100 + 100 + 500 = 700 E | ≈ 2.0 h | ≈ 30 min | none — the same instrument as P7.1's cells |

**Few-shot half, (a)–(e) with the cheap anchor: ≈ 13.5 h sequential, ≈ 3.3 h at 8 workers, plus 10–100 min of GPU training.**
**Whole P7.3 including the zero-shot arm (4 prompts × 2 subjects × 5 seeds × 100 draws = 4,000 E ≈ 11.7 h sequential / 2.7 h at 8 workers): ≈ 25 h sequential, ≈ 6 h at 8 workers, plus training.** In `tmux`, either way.

## 4. What must be decided or declared before `BRIEF_37`, because the cost depends on it

1. **The full-retrain anchor's corpus** (touches §4 C3's registered design): the DT on 200 MaxPressure SUMO episodes (cheap, consistent with the few-shot data, and the natural k = 200 endpoint of the same curve) or a SUMO-trained MAPPO (2–3 days of work and a design problem, `DEFERRED` 75(e)). **Recommendation: the MaxPressure-corpus DT, declared as the curve's endpoint; the paper says the anchor is a target-domain DT on probe data, not a target-domain online policy.**
2. **The fine-tune budget B in gradient steps**, one number for all k and both subjects, declared before the first run (§6.1). Its cost range is 10–100 min; its *scientific* choice is what needs registering.
3. **The SUMO evaluation seed rule** per held-out draw (`reset(seed=1000)` on a fresh env per draw, mirroring the probe, is the obvious twin).
4. **Parallelism at 8 workers is a measured ×4.9, not ×8**; schedule from 2.4 s per effective episode and re-run the canary before every campaign.

## Working notes — not part of the registered row

**Why the clock origin is a genuine third component and not an artefact of our instrument.** The engine
starts a vehicle's clock when it enters `vehiclePool`, which is when it begins queueing to be inserted.
Our metric starts it at admission to a lane. **The difference is real elapsed time that a vehicle spends
waiting to enter the network**, and it is exactly the quantity T1's B1 says our metric discards. The
decomposition therefore does not merely explain a discrepancy between two implementations — **it
separates a modelling choice (do vehicles waiting to enter count as travelling?) from a measurement
artefact (the 10 s decision grid), and those deserve different treatment in the paper.**

**What the decomposition does NOT establish.** It is measured on 46 episodes across 7 tiers and 2
scenarios. **`cf_cologne3` is not covered**, and the three components' relative sizes are scenario- and
policy-dependent — the cadence term alone was measured at a median of 4.19 on hz1x1 against 7.07 on
grid4x4 in `p8_4a_admission.json`, and nobody has explained that gap.

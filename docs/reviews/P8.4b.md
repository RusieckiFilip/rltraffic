### BL-2 — `n_escalations`, `n_pooling_dependent` and the "BOTH poolings" ruling are structurally unfalsifiable in the shipped artifact

1. **`pool_contrast` is dead code with respect to production.** It is called only from
   `tests/test_att_rederivation.py` (5 sites). `main()` never calls it; `evaluate_all_verdicts` never
   calls it. Every `PooledContrast`, the `escalate` flag, `POOLINGS` and the 2026-08-31 ruling *"both
   poolings reported and neither chosen"* live in a function the campaign does not execute.
2. **No contrast in `docs/data/p8_4b_rederivation.json` carries a `pooled` block.** All eight carry
   `by_definition` only. In `rederivation_artifact` (`:1722-1732`), `outcomes[definition]` is therefore
   always a singleton, so `pooling_dependent = [d for d,v in outcomes.items() if len(v) > 1]` **can never
   be non-empty**. `n_pooling_dependent = 0` is vacuous, and *"none is pooling-dependent"* is a statement
   that could not have come out otherwise.
3. **The escalation machinery is untested.** `n_escalations` is asserted exactly once in the whole test
   file (`:685`) and asserted to be **`0`**. Mutating `if pooling_dependent or definition_dependent:` →
   `if False:` (`:1735`) → **SURVIVED, 45 passed.** Mutating `definition_dependent` → `False` →
   **SURVIVED.**

A reviewer of the paper reading *"zero escalations"* would take it as a measurement that could have come
out otherwise. On the shipped artifact, one half of it (pooling) **could not**, and the other half
(definition) is unprotected by any test.

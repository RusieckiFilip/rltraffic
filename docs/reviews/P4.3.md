## 2. A2 — a mechanism for the headline, not a threat to it

The ten arms are ten configurations (own target, canary starting exactly at it, `rtg_scale` 9991.0
everywhere, no two arms with identical per-draw vectors). But the striking part is **how the effect
decays with prompt distance**:

| ΔT | pair | byte-identical ATT cells |
|---|---|---|
| 468 | Rule A (−12532) vs −13000 | **291/500** |
| 1000 | 0 vs −1000 | **189/500** |
| 1738 | −5762 vs −7500 | 42/500 |
| 13000 | 0 vs −13000 | 1/500 |

**At ΔT = 1000 the prompt changes nothing at all on ~38 % of episodes.** That is *why* the lever is
weak — the conditioning frequently does not move the argmax — and it belongs in the paper as the
mechanism behind the 0.9026 range.

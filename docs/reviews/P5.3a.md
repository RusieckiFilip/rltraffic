## BLOCKERS

### BL-1 — the packet's key qualitative claim is contradicted by the committed artifact

`docs/returns/P5.3a.md:241-244` and the `fixedtime` row at `:172` state: *"That conjunction holds for
**`fixedtime` alone** (flip 0.000000 **and** TVD 0.000000 on every intervention). For **`random`** the
flip rate is 0 … but the TVD is **not identically zero**."*

**Re-measured by the coordinator from `docs/data/p5_3a_rtg_probe.json`, 2026-08-25:**

| tier | TVD range over 11 interventions × 5 seeds | `mean_abs_logit_delta` | TVD exactly 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fixedtime` | **4.904e−11 … 2.271e−09** | 0.0360 … 0.2082 | **0 of 55** |
| `random` | 1.433e−07 … 5.465e−05 | 0.0611 … 0.2553 | 0 of 55 |
| `mappo1000` | 0.0 … 6.047e−03 | 0.0 … 0.4934 | **5 of 55** |

**`fixedtime`'s TVD is never zero; it is a six-decimal rounding of ~1e−9.** Its
`mean_abs_logit_delta` reaches 0.2082 — **the same order as `random`'s 0.2553**. So the stated
discriminator (*`fixedtime` exactly zero, `random` not*) **does not exist in the data**. Both tiers
move their logits comparably and neither crosses a decision boundary.

⚠️ **This is the sentence `BRIEF_28` §8 item 2 was written to fence:** *"Do not write 'the token is
inert' unless the flip rate is 0 across the whole grid AND the TVD is negligible."* The packet used a
non-existent exact zero to license the inert reading on exactly one tier. **The conclusion may well
survive — 1e−9 is negligible — but the stated basis is false, and the real discriminator is a
four-orders-of-magnitude TVD gap at comparable logit movement, which is a different and more
interesting fact.** ⭐ **Coordinator's note: the only exact-zero TVDs in the entire artifact are
`mappo1000`'s five — which are R6's null control, one per seed. The artifact's one true zero is the
one the packet does not point at.**

Also: the `random` TVD range quoted in prose (*"3e−6 … 3.3e−5"*) is the **`grid_g8` column only**;
across all interventions it is 1.4e−7 … 5.5e−5.
